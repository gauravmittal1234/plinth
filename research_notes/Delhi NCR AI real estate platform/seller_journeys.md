# Seller Journeys and Pain Points in Delhi NCR (2026): (A) individual resale-flat sellers and (B) developers selling new projects

Research notes compiled 17 Oct 2026. Scope: Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad. Detailed regulation (RERA mechanics, stamp duty schedules) is covered by a separate researcher and appears here only where it shapes the seller's experience.

Method and source-quality note: much of the operational detail (brokerage norms, funnel benchmarks, CPL figures, AI tools) comes from 2026 blogs written by brokers, proptech vendors and CRM companies. They are flagged as "vendor/broker blog" inline because they have a commercial interest and rarely cite underlying data. Market-size data comes from ANAROCK (Q3 2026 Pan-India Viewpoints; NCR report April 2025) and ICRA (Dec 2025). Several Business Standard pages returned HTTP 403, so for those only headline or search-snippet facts are used, and they are marked as such. The session's web-search budget ran out partway through, so some planned lookups (developer annual-report expense lines, Reddit seller threads, MagicBricks/Housing.com seller plans, circle-rate/cash data) could not be done. Those are listed under Gaps.

## A1. What does the resale seller's journey look like, stage by stage (activities, stakeholders, costs, timelines, tools)?

### Takeaway
A Delhi NCR resale sale typically runs through a broker-led funnel: pricing based on hearsay, a listing on a portal and/or with local dealers, a token, an agreement to sell, then 6–10 weeks to registration when the buyer takes a loan. The seller carries most of the coordination work: clearing their own loan and getting the original documents back, collecting society, authority and builder NOCs, sorting out TDS, and arranging registration. The authority layer differs a lot by sub-market: Noida's Transfer Memorandum, DDA leasehold-to-freehold conversion in Delhi, and builder and society NOCs in Gurugram and Noida.

### Cited Findings
**Timelines**
- "Six to ten weeks from signed agreement to registration is typical for a financed transaction." A property priced at market "will attract inquiries within days in an active locality". Source is a Gurugram seller-service blog (vendor), 27 Jun 2026 — [BookPropertyVisit](https://bookpropertyvisit.com/blog/real-estate/how-to-sell-your-home-in-gurgaon-fast-and-pay-only-after-it-sells)
- A property listed for 3+ months "signal[s] seller's floor is negotiable". This is buyer-side advice from a broker, 7 Jul 2026 — [Square Yards](https://www.squareyards.com/blog/how-to-negotiate-property-price)
- Society NOC in Noida takes 7–15 working days and costs ₹500–₹2,000 plus any pending dues. It is described as mandatory before registry under the UP Apartment Owners Act 2010 (developer blog, undated) — [Bhavishya Nirman](https://bhavishyanirman.com/blog/is-society-noc-required-for-sale-of-flat-in-noida/). A NoBroker search snippet gives the same figures — [NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/society-transfer-charges/)
- A real-estate lawyer "can review all documents in 2–5 days" (Gurugram resale guide, 28 May 2026) — [100acress](https://www.100acress.com/blog/buying-a-resale-flat-in-gurgaon-what-homebuyers-should-know/)

**Stakeholders and documents, by stage**
- Pricing/listing: owners can post one free listing on 99acres. Paid plans add visibility, expert-written descriptions, email promotion and a relationship manager — [99acres seller guide](https://www.99acres.com/articles/using-99acres-to-sell-your-property.html) (via search summary). NoBroker's paid seller plans include an RM, Facebook ads, a hidden phone number, and on the "Super" tiers a photoshoot and a field assistant for showings — [NoBroker, updated 2 Jul 2026](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/nobroker-seller-plans)
- Brokerage terms: the fee, GST and the stage at which it is paid should be "agreed in writing before the first site visit". Payment should be tied to "the agreement or the registry, not the token" — [RealBetter Gurgaon, 2026](https://gurgaon.realbetter.com/blog/charges/brokerage-charges-in-gurgaon). Brokerage is "usually [paid] at the time of the agreement or registration" — [RealtyHunting, 2 Aug 2026](https://realtyhunting.com/brokerage-charges-in-real-estate-india/)
- Documents buyers ask a Gurugram seller for: original sale deed, latest encumbrance certificate, OC and CC, Society/RWA NOC (28 Nov 2025) — [Inframantra](https://inframantra.com/blog/things-you-need-to-know-before-buying-a-resale-flat-in-gurgaon). Buyers are also told to "review all ownership transfer documents from the first allotment till now", get written no-dues for maintenance, utilities and property tax, and confirm the seller's home loan is closed — [100acress](https://www.100acress.com/blog/buying-a-resale-flat-in-gurgaon-what-homebuyers-should-know/)
- Loan closure and document release: the RBI directs lenders to "release all the original movable or immovable property documents and remove charges registered with any registry within a period of 30 days after full repayment/settlement". Lenders pay ₹5,000 per day of delay if the delay is their fault. If documents are lost, the lender has 30 more days (60 total) and must help get certified copies at its own cost. The borrower chooses which branch or office to collect from — [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/release-property-documents-within-30-days-on-repayment-or-settlement-of-loans-else-pay-rs5000-per-day-as-compensation-rbi/71983.html). These rules apply from 1 Dec 2023 — [Moneylife search snippet](https://www.moneylife.in/article/release-property-documents-within-30-days-on-repayment-or-settlement-of-loans-else-pay-rs5000-per-day-as-compensation-rbi/71983.html); RBI announced them 13 Sep 2023 — [News on AIR](https://newsonair.gov.in/rbi-asks-financial-institutions-to-release-all-movable-immovable-property-documents-within-30-days-of-full-repayment-of-loan)
- Noida authority step: the buyer must submit the sale deed and documents to Noida Authority with the Transfer Memorandum (TM) charge, then wait for the TM certificate. "Without that certificate, the flat's yours on paper but not in the Authority's books" (Outlook Money, 7 Aug 2025) — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/real-estate/transfer-memorandum-the-hidden-tax-on-resale-flats-in-noida)
- TDS step: the buyer deducts 1% (resident seller, consideration ≥ ₹50 lakh) and deposits it via Form 26QB (Form 141 from 1 Apr 2026) within 30 days of the end of the month. The seller gets Form 16B (Form 132 from 1 Apr 2026) — [TaxGarden, updated 22 Sep 2026](https://taxgarden.in/blog/tds-on-property-purchase-section-194-ia-form-26qb-india)

**Costs borne by or affecting the seller**
- Seller-side brokerage is about 1% plus 18% GST on the fee. Example: ₹2.2 cr builder floor × 1% = ₹2.2 lakh + ₹39,600 GST = ₹2,59,600 — [RealBetter Gurgaon](https://gurgaon.realbetter.com/blog/charges/brokerage-charges-in-gurgaon)
- Optional platform plan: NoBroker ₹7,499–₹20,999 + GST — [NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/nobroker-seller-plans)
- Noida TM charge is paid by the buyer but changes how much the buyer can pay. It ranges from 2.5% to 50% of the original allotment price depending on allotment year, plus fixed surcharges (MIG ₹1 lakh, HIG ₹1.5 lakh). The charge doubles for GPA transfers — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/real-estate/transfer-memorandum-the-hidden-tax-on-resale-flats-in-noida)
- DDA conversion (Delhi): conversion charges can be paid as a lump sum or in instalments over up to 5 years at 12% interest. Expired leases also need a one-time lease renewal charge of ₹1,000/sq m and ground rent at 5× — [DDA conversion brochure (2014, older)](https://dda.gov.in/sites/default/files/Housing%20Department/Conversion%20Brochure%20Modified-2014.pdf); [99acres](https://www.99acres.com/articles/how-to-convert-leasehold-to-freehold-in-delhi.html) (search summary)

## A2. What are brokerage norms in Delhi NCR, and how do local brokers and property dealers behave (dual agency, cartels)?

### Takeaway
The resale norm in NCR is about 1% from each side, with some agents asking up to 2%. GST at 18% applies to the fee, and fees are paid at agreement or registration. Dual agency (one dealer collecting about 1% from both sides) is common enough that guides warn about it explicitly. No fixed legal rate exists. I found no sourced evidence on "broker cartels" in societies; it needs primary research.

### Cited Findings
- Resale in Gurugram: about 1% from each side. Buyer-side agents sometimes quote up to 2%. GST at 18% is charged "on the agent's fee, never on the property price" (2026) — [RealBetter Gurgaon](https://gurgaon.realbetter.com/blog/charges/brokerage-charges-in-gurgaon)
- Dual agency: when one agent represents both parties they "usually collect a fee from each". Example: ₹3 lakh before GST on a ₹1.5 cr deal at 1% per side — [RealBetter Gurgaon](https://gurgaon.realbetter.com/blog/charges/brokerage-charges-in-gurgaon)
- India-wide guide: "1% to 2% of the property price"; "often both buyer and seller pay their own agent"; "ask upfront who is paying the agent, so you are not billed twice"; "there is no fixed legal brokerage rate in India" (2 Aug 2026) — [RealtyHunting](https://realtyhunting.com/brokerage-charges-in-real-estate-india/)
- Rentals in Gurugram: half to one month's rent from the tenant — [RealBetter Gurgaon](https://gurgaon.realbetter.com/blog/charges/brokerage-charges-in-gurgaon)
- Agents dealing in RERA-registered projects must register with HRERA. The penalty is up to ₹10,000 per day of default, capped at 5% of property cost — [RealBetter Gurgaon](https://gurgaon.realbetter.com/blog/charges/brokerage-charges-in-gurgaon). RERA registration is "mandatory for channel partners" — [Leadrat, 9 Jun 2026](https://leadrat.com/channel-partners-in-real-estate-a-complete-2026-guide/)
- Portals monetise brokers' access to owners. 99acres' "BOSS" (Broker Owner Supply Solutions) plan, from ₹3,149/month, lets brokers "unlock contact details of up to 50 homeowners interested to sell their properties in your chosen locality" (Jun 2024, older) — [Privyr](https://www.privyr.com/blog/are-99acres-lead-generation-packages-worth-the-investment/)
- Broker pressure on sellers: sellers face "a buyer's lowball offer driven by broker pressure" and "hosting unproductive walk-throughs" (vendor blog, Jun 2026) — [BookPropertyVisit](https://bookpropertyvisit.com/blog/real-estate/how-to-sell-your-home-in-gurgaon-fast-and-pay-only-after-it-sells)

### Inferences
- On a ₹1.5–2.5 cr Gurugram/Noida flat, seller-side brokerage plus GST is about ₹1.8–3 lakh. Dual agency doubles the dealer's take and means one agent controls both price discovery and information flow. That conflict is a clear wedge for a seller-side AI agent that pre-qualifies buyers and documents comparable prices.
- Because portals sell owner contact data to brokers (99acres BOSS), listing as an "owner" on a portal can itself cause broker solicitation. This likely explains the "spam" complaints in A6.

### Gaps
- No source found on "broker cartels" or society-level gatekeeping (for example, dealers stationed at gated societies who control visits or steer buyers). Primary interviews or Reddit/Quora mining are needed; the search budget ran out before this could be checked.
- No data on the share of NCR resale deals closed via broker vs direct owner-to-buyer, or exclusive vs open mandates.
- Delhi- and Noida-specific brokerage norms were not separately sourced. The Gurugram figures are assumed to be broadly representative.

## A3. How long does it take to sell a resale flat in NCR, what share never sells, and what is the typical negotiation discount?

### Takeaway
No reliable NCR-specific resale days-on-market or "never sells" statistic was found. The often-quoted ICRA figure of 0.6 years-to-sell refers to developer (primary) inventory, not resale. Negotiation discounts from broker guides are 2–7% for non-urgent sellers and 5–15% for motivated sellers. A verified Noida example closed 7.6% below asking.

### Cited Findings
- Negotiation ranges (Square Yards, 7 Jul 2026, broker blog with no external data): motivated individual resale seller 5–15% off asking; non-urgent 2–7%; developer new launch 1–4% on headline price plus ₹2–5 lakh in add-ons — [Square Yards](https://www.squareyards.com/blog/how-to-negotiate-property-price)
- Noida case: listed ₹92 lakh; comparables ₹83–85 lakh in a 4-month window; closed at ₹85 lakh (7.6% below asking) — [Square Yards](https://www.squareyards.com/blog/how-to-negotiate-property-price). Gurugram example: 3BHK asking ₹1.45 cr against registered comparables at ₹1.33 cr and ₹1.37 cr (search snippet) — [Square Yards](https://www.squareyards.com/blog/tips-to-buy-property-at-low-price)
- Primary-market (developer) years-to-sell in NCR fell to 0.6 years by Sept 2025 from 1.7 years in March 2022, and ICRA expects 0.6–0.7 years through March 2027 (ICRA, Dec 2025) — [ICRA](https://www.icra.in/Rating/DownloadResearchSummaryReport?id=6688). Note: this is new-supply inventory, not resale.
- NCR primary inventory overhang: 18 months in Q3 2026, down 7% YoY — [ANAROCK Q3 2026](https://websitemedia.anarock.com/media/Q3_2026_Pan_India_Residential_Market_Viewpoints_9cf1a7e616.pdf)
- Price momentum affects how sellers anchor. NCR average base selling price (primary) was ₹9,980/sq ft in Q3 2026, +12% YoY and the highest appreciation among the top 7 cities — [ANAROCK Q3 2026](https://websitemedia.anarock.com/media/Q3_2026_Pan_India_Residential_Market_Viewpoints_9cf1a7e616.pdf). NCR prices rose more than 30% in 2024 — [ANAROCK NCR report, Apr 2025](https://websitemedia.anarock.com/media/NCR_Real_Estate_2025_87aca82f87.pdf)
- Overpriced listings are "ignored by serious buyers" and used by agents as comparison tools — [BookPropertyVisit](https://bookpropertyvisit.com/blog/real-estate/how-to-sell-your-home-in-gurgaon-fast-and-pay-only-after-it-sells)

### Inferences
- With primary prices up 30%+ in 2024 and 12% YoY in Q3 2026, resale sellers are likely to anchor on new-launch rates or portal asking prices instead of registered resale transactions. That produces the 5–15% gap that comparables-driven buyers exploit. An AI pricing product built on registered-deed data (where it can be obtained) is a strong seller-side offer.
- The fact that a "3+ months on market" signal is used by buyers suggests a typical healthy NCR resale sale takes about 1–3 months to a token plus 6–10 weeks to registration (about 3–5 months end to end). This is an inference, not measured.

### Gaps
- No credible NCR resale days-on-market, listing-to-sale conversion, or "share of listings that never sell" data was found. Portals (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing, NoBroker) hold this data but did not surface any public figures within this search budget.
- No data on resale's share of total NCR transactions (registrations) for 2025–26.

## A4. What tax and money issues do resale sellers face (LTCG post-July 2024, 54/54EC, TDS 194-IA, NRI TDS/195 and lower-TDS certificates, cash component)?

### Takeaway
Since 23 July 2024, LTCG on property is 12.5% without indexation. Resident individuals and HUFs selling property bought before that date can instead choose 20% with indexation (the "grandfathering" option), which means they have to work out two calculations. Buyers deduct 1% TDS on deals of ₹50 lakh or more. NRI sellers face much heavier withholding under section 195 unless they get a lower-deduction certificate, which is a known bottleneck. From 1 April 2026 the Income-tax Act, 2025 renumbers these sections (194-IA/195 → 393(1)/(2)) and the forms (26QB → 141), which adds confusion.

### Cited Findings
**LTCG**
- For property held over 24 months: "the tax rate of 12.5% will be applicable, without indexation benefit" or alternatively "20% tax rate with indexation benefit is available" for property acquired on or before 22 July 2024 — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/long-term-capital-gains-ltcg-tax). Note: ClearTax uses the word "grandfathering" only for the 2018 equity rule under 112A, but the two-option property rule it describes is the same as below.
- The option is limited to resident individuals and HUFs for land or buildings acquired before 23 July 2024. Tax is the lower of 12.5% without indexation and 20% with indexation. NRIs, companies, LLPs and firms do not qualify (search summaries) — [Finnovate](https://www.finnovate.in/learn/blog/capital-gains-tax-on-property-india-explained); [Bajaj Finserv](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/investments/long-term-capital-gain-tax-on-property)
- Holding of 24 months or less is short-term, taxed at slab rates — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/long-term-capital-gains-ltcg-tax)

**Exemptions**
- Section 54: reinvest in another residential property; exemption capped at ₹10 crore. Unused amounts must be deposited in the Capital Gains Account Scheme by the return due date — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/long-term-capital-gains-ltcg-tax)
- Section 54EC: up to ₹50 lakh in NHAI, REC, PFC or IRFC bonds (within 6 months) — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/long-term-capital-gains-ltcg-tax)

**TDS on resident sellers (buyer's obligation, seller's cash flow)**
- 1% TDS when consideration is ₹50 lakh or more. The base is the higher of agreement value or stamp-duty value (since 1 Apr 2022) and includes parking, club, maintenance advance and floor-rise charges. It rises to 20% if the seller's PAN is not furnished. Since 1 Oct 2024 the ₹50 lakh threshold applies to aggregate consideration across joint buyers and sellers — [TaxGarden, updated 22 Sep 2026](https://taxgarden.in/blog/tds-on-property-purchase-section-194-ia-form-26qb-india)
- Form 26QB (Form 141 from 1 Apr 2026) is due within 30 days from the end of the month of deduction. The buyer issues Form 16B (Form 132 from 1 Apr 2026) within 15 days after that. No TAN is needed — [TaxGarden](https://taxgarden.in/blog/tds-on-property-purchase-section-194-ia-form-26qb-india); [Tax2win](https://tax2win.in/guide/form-26qb)
- Under the Income-tax Act 2025 (from 1 Apr 2026) the obligation moves to Section 393(1) for resident sellers and Section 393(2) for NRI sellers, with "identical rates and thresholds" — [TaxGarden](https://taxgarden.in/blog/tds-on-property-purchase-section-194-ia-form-26qb-india)

**NRI sellers**
- Section 195 (now 393(2)) applies, not 194-IA. The LTCG rate on transfers from 23 Jul 2024 is 12.5% plus surcharge and cess. TaxGarden states an effective figure of "20.6% with surcharge and cess", which does not reconcile with 12.5% plus standard surcharge and cess and should be verified. STCG is 30%. The buyer needs a TAN (limited exceptions from 1 Oct 2026) and files Form 27Q quarterly. A Lower Deduction Certificate (Section 197) can reduce withholding — [TaxGarden](https://taxgarden.in/blog/tds-on-property-purchase-section-194-ia-form-26qb-india)
- NRIs cannot use Form 26QB — [Tax2win/ search summary](https://tax2win.in/guide/section-194ia-under-income-tax-act)
- NRI exit demand in 2026: Business Standard headlines (29–31 Jul 2026) report "46% of NRI property owners want to sell now; 26% plan exits within 6 months" and "89% of NRI property exits are residential; Maharashtra tops resale listings" (pages returned 403, headline only) — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/46-of-nri-property-owners-want-to-sell-now-26-plan-exits-within-6-months-126072900685_1.html); [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/personal-finance/89-of-nri-property-exits-are-residential-maharashtra-tops-resale-listings-126073100221_1.html)
- NoBroker markets NRI services including "TDS compliance on property sales, lower deduction certificate applications, capital gains advisory and repatriation assistance", POA drafting and registration. A competitor reviewer (66 MG Road, disclosed) criticises the lack of public pricing and accountability commitments (11 Jun 2026, updated 12 Oct 2026) — [66 MG Road](https://66mgroad.com/guides/nobroker-nri-services-review)

### Inferences
- For a typical NCR seller who bought in 2010–2018, the two-way LTCG comparison (12.5% flat vs 20% indexed) plus 54/54EC planning is a high-value, easy-to-automate calculator. It also creates natural lead-in points for the platform (reinvestment purchase, CGAS deposit, bond purchase).
- For NRI sellers, the gap between about 12.5%+ withholding on the full sale consideration and the actual tax on the gain traps cash for months unless a lower-deduction certificate is obtained before registration. That makes LDC application tracking a strong paid service, given NRI exit intent in 2026.
- The April 2026 renumbering (Section 393, Forms 141/132) is likely to cause buyer-side errors in the first year (wrong form, late filing), which can lead to TDS credit mismatches for sellers. This is inferred; there is no data on error rates.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on cash ("black"/"on-money") components in NCR resale, or on the circle-rate vs market-rate gaps in Delhi/Gurugram/Noida that drive it. Searches could not be completed.
- Lower-deduction-certificate turnaround times on TRACES were not found.
- Section numbers for 54/54EC under the Income-tax Act 2025 were not verified. The legacy numbering is used above.
- The effective NRI LTCG withholding rate with surcharge and cess (the 20.6% claim) needs confirmation from the Income Tax Department or a CA source.

## A5. What documentation and location-specific frictions affect NCR resale sellers (DDA leasehold, Noida TM, society NOC, chain documents, loan document release)?

### Takeaway
NCR resale friction depends on location. Delhi DDA flats may need leasehold-to-freehold conversion and clearance of all dues. Noida/Greater Noida leasehold flats need a Noida Authority Transfer Memorandum (paid by the buyer, but it lowers what the buyer will pay) and a society NOC. Large numbers of Noida/Greater Noida buyers in legacy projects still lack registry or possession, so their units are hard to resell. For mortgaged flats, release of original documents is now time-bound by RBI, but sellers still have to chase it.

### Cited Findings
- Noida TM rates as a percentage of original allotment price: pre-1990 50%; 1991–2000 20%; 2001–2011 10%; 2011 onwards 5%; post-24 Sept 2021 allotments 2.5%. Plus fixed surcharges (EWS ₹30k, LIG ₹60k, MIG ₹1 lakh, HIG ₹1.5 lakh). The buyer pays. Family transfers get 50% relief or exemption. "GPA transfers: charges double" (Aug 2025) — [Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/real-estate/transfer-memorandum-the-hidden-tax-on-resale-flats-in-noida)
- Noida resale needs NOCs from Noida Authority, society, and the bank where a loan exists (search summary) — [NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/transfer-charges-in-noida/)
- Society NOC in Noida is delayed by incomplete documentation, outstanding maintenance, pending membership verification, resident ownership disputes and committee procedures — [Bhavishya Nirman](https://bhavishyanirman.com/blog/is-society-noc-required-for-sale-of-flat-in-noida/)
- DDA: conversion to freehold "makes the owner eligible to transfer the flat without seeking the consent of any third party" and freehold flats sell at higher prices. Conversion happens only after all instalments, ground rent, misuse charges and damages are cleared (search summary) — [99acres](https://www.99acres.com/articles/how-to-convert-leasehold-to-freehold-in-delhi.html); [DDA brochure, 2014](https://dda.gov.in/sites/default/files/Housing%20Department/Conversion%20Brochure%20Modified-2014.pdf)
- Legacy stuck stock in Gautam Buddh Nagar (Nov 2023, older): 35% of India's stalled residential projects are in Noida/Greater Noida, about 1.65 lakh units worth ₹1.18 lakh cr. At least 1 lakh people await flat registration, about 60,000 lack possession past the promised date, and 27,992 UP RERA complaints had been filed (as of 2020) — [PSU Watch / Noida Dialogue white paper](https://psuwatch.com/press-releases/ncr-stalled-homes-whitepaper)
- UP stalled projects (Business Standard, Sep 2024, search snippet): Greater Noida 167 projects / 74,645 units; Noida 103 projects / 41,438 units; 378 projects / about 1.46 lakh units across 5 UP markets — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/housing-crisis-stalled-projects-surge-in-up-greater-noida-and-noida-lead-124090900283_1.html)
- RBI: originals plus charge removal within 30 days, ₹5,000/day compensation, and 60 days if documents are lost — [Moneylife](https://www.moneylife.in/article/release-property-documents-within-30-days-on-repayment-or-settlement-of-loans-else-pay-rs5000-per-day-as-compensation-rbi/71983.html)

### Inferences
- A seller-side "document readiness score" covering chain documents from first allotment, lien status, society dues, TM liability (for pricing), DDA freehold status, and builder registry/OC status would directly address the biggest causes of failed or delayed deals. It could also improve pricing, because TM and conversion costs reduce what buyers will pay.
- Noida/Greater Noida owners in projects without registry are a distinct, under-served segment. They can often only sell via builder-endorsed transfers, so they need specialist handling. This is inferred from the white-paper scale; no direct resale data was found.

- Mutation in Delhi MCD and lost-document reconstruction (FIR, newspaper ad, indemnity) were not sourced.

## B1. What is the NCR new-home market context in 2026 that shapes developer selling?

### Takeaway
NCR is a high-price, luxury-skewed, relatively tight market. In Q3 2026, ANAROCK recorded 13,750 units sold, 10,900 launched, 86,200 units of available inventory (about 18 months overhang) and average prices of ₹9,980/sq ft, up 12% YoY and the fastest growth among the top 7 cities. At the same time, NCR (especially Noida/Greater Noida) carries the country's worst legacy of stalled projects, which drives a deep buyer trust deficit.

### Cited Findings
- Q3 2026 NCR (ANAROCK): launches 10,900 (−3% QoQ, −14% YoY); sales 13,750 (+3% QoQ, −1% YoY); available inventory 86,200 (−3% QoQ, −2% YoY; about 14% of top-7 stock); average base selling price ₹9,980/sq ft (+2% QoQ, +12% YoY); inventory overhang 18 months (−7% YoY) — [ANAROCK Q3 2026](https://websitemedia.anarock.com/media/Q3_2026_Pan_India_Residential_Market_Viewpoints_9cf1a7e616.pdf)

## B5. What does the post-booking journey look like for developers (payments, updates, RERA, possession, handover)?

### Takeaway
Very little post-booking material was sourced in this round. What is evidenced: CP commission is tied to collection milestones with clawback on cancellation; collections, not bookings, drive developer cash flow (DLF collected a record ₹5,100 cr in a quarter when bookings were ₹419 cr); and the NCR legacy of delayed registry and possession (about 1 lakh awaiting registry in Noida/Greater Noida as of 2023) is the reason buyers distrust developers.

### Cited Findings
- CP payouts follow booking amount, agreement registration and a collection % threshold. "Clawback on cancellation is standard" — [Sell.Do](https://www.sell.do/blog/role-and-advantages-of-a-channel-partner-in-real-estate)
- DLF Q3 FY26: pre-sales ₹419 cr vs record collections ₹5,100 cr (+64% YoY). Dahlias bookings were paused for redesign "for enhanced customer experience" — [ICICI Direct](https://www.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_dlf_q3fy26.pdf)
- Signature Global Q2 FY27 collections about ₹1,010 cr (+51% QoQ) — [Screener](https://www.screener.in/company/SIGNATURE/consolidated/)
- Legacy delivery failure: about 1 lakh people awaiting registration, about 60,000 without possession past due date, and buyers paying EMI plus rent at the same time (Noida/Greater Noida, Nov 2023) — [PSU Watch](https://psuwatch.com/press-releases/ncr-stalled-homes-whitepaper)

### Inferences
Synthesized post-booking stage map. Items marked [unsourced] come from domain knowledge and should be verified.

| Stage | Developer activity | Buyer touchpoint | Pain point | AI opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Booking & KYC | Application form, booking amount, KYC, allotment letter | Sales/CRM team | Manual KYC, CP attribution disputes | Auto-KYC, attribution ledger |
| Agreement for sale | Draft BBA as per RERA, registration of agreement [unsourced in NCR detail] | Legal/CRM | Delays and queries | Doc Q&A agent |

| Possession | OC, final demand, possession letter, snagging, registry | CRM/site team | Snag lists, registry delays | Snagging app with AI defect tagging |
| Handover to RWA | Maintenance agency, corpus transfer | Facility team | Disputes over maintenance and corpus | Ticketing, transparency |

## B6. What are the key pain points for developers selling new projects?

### Takeaway
The evidenced pain points are lead leakage and slow response, duplicate leads and CP attribution disputes, CP dependency and payout friction, buyer trust deficit from NCR's stalled-project legacy, demand softening in some segments (Signature Global FY26 bookings −20%), and RERA advertising compliance enforcement (UP-RERA fined 91 developers in Jan 2025).

### Cited Findings
- Lead leakage: "Most developers and brokers lose deals not because leads dry up, but because leads leak: an enquiry that never got called back, a hot buyer who cooled while waiting for a follow-up, a site visit that was booked and then forgotten" — [Sell.Do](https://www.sell.do/blog/real-estate-crm-lead-management-guide)
- Response time: portal leads average 4–6 hours to first contact; "80% of sales require 5+ follow-up touches" while agents average 1.3. Without CRM, about 75% of leads are never reached (vendor, unsourced data) — [Realatic](https://realatic.com/blog/real-estate-lead-conversion-india/). "68% of real estate inquiries in India happen after business hours" (vendor claim) — [Qreo/dev.to](https://dev.to/qreo-digital/how-ai-voice-agents-are-changing-real-estate-lead-follow-up-in-india-2026-3g6n)
- CP frictions: lead ownership disputes ("first registered source owns the lead for a defined window"), attribution breakdown, internal leakage via "WhatsApp, personal phones, and paper", and payout opacity that makes partners "deprioritize projects" — [Sell.Do](https://www.sell.do/blog/role-and-advantages-of-a-channel-partner-in-real-estate)
- Demand softening for some: Signature Global FY26 bookings fell 20% to ₹8,220 cr vs its ₹12,500 cr target (search snippet) — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/signature-global-reports-5-drop-in-q4-sales-bookings-on-weak-demand-126040900776_1.html); [Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/signature-global-aims-215-rise-in-sales-bookings-to-rs-12500-crore-in-fy26-on-strong-demand). NCR sales −1% YoY and launches −14% YoY in Q3 2026 — [ANAROCK](https://websitemedia.anarock.com/media/Q3_2026_Pan_India_Residential_Market_Viewpoints_9cf1a7e616.pdf)
- RERA advertising compliance: on 27 Jan 2025 UP-RERA fined 91 developers ₹2.66 cr for advertising without registration or without the RERA number/QR code. Those named include ATS Realty, Ansal Housing, Antriksh Realtech, AIMS Promoters and Wave Mega City Centre — [Best Media Info](https://bestmediainfo.com/mediainfo/mediainfo-marketing/up-rera-imposes-rs-266-crore-fine-on-91-developers-for-violating-ad-norms-8656562). (For comparison, MahaRERA's Apr 2025 circular requires the QR code and number in the top-right quadrant, with fines up to ₹50,000 — [Real Estate Law Journal](https://realestatelawjournal.in/maharera-qr-code-registration-ad-guidelines-2025/).)
- Trust deficit: ANAROCK describes NCR's history of abandoned projects, pre-launch-scheme pyramids and fund diversion — [ANAROCK NCR 2025](https://websitemedia.anarock.com/media/NCR_Real_Estate_2025_87aca82f87.pdf)
- Product/design risk: DLF paused Dahlias bookings for a quarter to redesign — [ICICI Direct](https://www.icicidirect.com/mailcontent/idirect_dlf_q3fy26.pdf)

### Inferences
- With 91 developers fined in UP alone, compliance-checking of every creative (RERA number, QR code, carpet-area disclosures) before it runs on Meta/Google/portals is a low-risk, high-frequency AI use case that also applies to CPs, who often create their own ads.
- CP attribution and payout transparency is a two-sided product opportunity: developer-side deduplication plus a CP-side earnings ledger. It ties directly to the commission spend, which is the largest acquisition cost.

### Gaps
- No sourced data on cancellation rates, duplicate-lead share, or fake-lead share for NCR developers.
- HRERA (Haryana) advertising enforcement actions were not found (UP-RERA only).
- No sourced data on unsold ready inventory by developer tier.

## B8. How do developers use AI today (voice AI, WhatsApp bots, lead scoring), and what is their appetite?

### Takeaway
2026 evidence is almost entirely vendor marketing. Voice-AI and WhatsApp-bot vendors target Indian real estate with Hinglish lead qualification and site-visit booking at about ₹2.7–6 per minute or about ₹8,000–15,000 a month, pitched as replacing ₹15,000–25,000/month telecallers. Named deployments are rare: NoBroker reportedly uses Convozen at scale, and ANAROCK has an "ANAROCK AI" analytics brand. No neutral survey of NCR developer AI adoption was found.

### Cited Findings
- Use cases: AI voice agents ask budget, location, BHK and timeline questions, score leads, book site visits into calendars, and send WhatsApp confirmations. Claimed response time drops "from 4+ hours to under 60 seconds" and "lead qualification rate improves by 3x" (vendor, 31 May 2026). Costs: DIY ₹8,000–15,000/month; agency ₹25,000–50,000 setup + ₹8,000/month; vs telecaller ₹15,000–25,000/month. Compliance: TRAI guidelines and the DPDP Act 2023 require opt-in and DND scrubbing — [Qreo/dev.to](https://dev.to/qreo-digital/how-ai-voice-agents-are-changing-real-estate-lead-follow-up-in-india-2026-3g6n)
- Vendor landscape (MyOperator, a vendor, 10 Oct 2026): pricing examples Bolti ₹6/min, Tabbly ₹2.7–3.9/min, Caller Digital ₹5–25 per connected call. Hindi/Hinglish code-switching is widely supported (MyOperator, Gnani, SquadStack, HuskyVoice). Vendors claim TRAI/UCC/DPDP compliance features. No named real-estate deployments — [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/top-10-voice-ai-agents-india-2026)
- Convozen AI (voice + WhatsApp) is "already used at scale by NoBroker"; CallQuants is positioned for "Hinglish voice calling, portal lead rescue, and site visit bookings" for Indian developers (search snippet; not fetched, and which page it came from is uncertain) — [Caller Digital](https://caller.digital/blog/top-10-voice-ai-agents-india-2026); [CallQuants](https://callquants.us/landing/ai-voice-agent-real-estate-india)
- ANAROCK uses an "Anarock AI" analysis brand for market insight (Aug 2025 headline) — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/finance/personal-finance/india-s-housing-sweet-spot-anarock-ai-says-it-s-50-lakh-1-cr-homes-125080801155_1.html)
- Salesforce markets "AI-driven insights" for lead management and forecasting to multi-project developers (vendor comparison) — [Sell.Do](https://www.sell.do/blog/top-crm-software-solutions-for-real-estate-developers-and-realtors)
- Square Yards' PropVR is described as an "AI-based 3D visualisation platform" — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_Yards_(company))

### Inferences
- Appetite is probably highest where AI directly replaces a measurable cost line (telecallers, missed portal leads after hours) and where response speed is shown to matter (vendor claims of 8–10x conversion within 5 minutes). This makes AI pre-sales the easiest entry point with developers. CP-attribution and RERA-ad-compliance AI are differentiated second products.
- Barriers to watch: TRAI commercial-communication rules and DND, DPDP consent for outbound AI calls, Hinglish accuracy, and integration with existing CRMs. A platform that ships as a CRM/WhatsApp add-on avoids replacing incumbent systems.

### Gaps
- No neutral survey (ANAROCK, JLL, Knight Frank, CREDAI, NAREDCO) on developer AI adoption or budgets was found within the search budget.
- No confirmed named NCR developer deployment of AI voice/WhatsApp agents (e.g., DLF, M3M, Godrej, Max, Signature).
- No data on AI lead-scoring accuracy or proven uplift in NCR. All uplift numbers above are vendor claims.
