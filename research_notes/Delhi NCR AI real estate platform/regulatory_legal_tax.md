## 1. RERA: central Act, Delhi RERA / HARERA / UP RERA, public portals, agent registration, advertisement and QR-code rules, recent orders

### Takeaway
RERA 2016 has been fully in force since 1 May 2017. It requires every qualifying project and every real estate agent to register with the state authority. It bars advertising or selling an unregistered project, and it imposes heavy daily penalties on unregistered agents. NCR regulators are active on advertising in 2024–2026. UP RERA made a project QR code mandatory in all ads and brochures (July 2024). HARERA Gurugram opened suo motu proceedings in April–May 2026 against a developer whose ads did not show the RERA number, the HARERA website or the DTCP licence number.

### Cited Findings
- The RERA Act was enacted on 25 March 2016. 61 of its 92 sections commenced on 1 May 2016 and the remaining 31 on 1 May 2017. — [Wikipedia: RERA Act 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Estate_(Regulation_and_Development)_Act,_2016)
- Core buyer protections:
  - 70% of buyer money must go into a dedicated bank account.
  - Prices must be quoted on carpet area.
  - There is a 5-year defect liability period.
  - Promoters who fail to register face fines of up to 10% of the estimated project cost.
  - Agents face ₹10,000 per day for non-compliance, capped at 5% of cost.
  - Agents need prior registration and get a unique registration number per state/UT.
  — [Wikipedia: RERA Act 2016](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_Estate_(Regulation_and_Development)_Act,_2016)
- Agent registration (secondary source):
  - Section 9(1) bars anyone from facilitating the sale or purchase of a plot, apartment or building in a registered project without registering as an agent.
  - Registration is granted by the state authority and is valid for 5 years.
  - Fees are roughly ₹10,000–25,000 depending on the state.
  - The certificate is issued in Form H.
  — [LegalServiceIndia: RERA agent registration guide](https://www.legalserviceindia.com/Legal-Articles/rera-agent-registration-india-guide/)
- **UP RERA QR-code mandate (reported 23 July 2024).**
  - Promoters must publish the project QR code in "every brochure and advertisement". — [Construction World](https://www.constructionworld.in/latest-construction-news/real-estate-news/up-rera-mandates-qr-codes-for-projects/59405/)
  - A second report says the requirement extends to booking forms, allotment letters, builder-buyer agreements (BBAs) and every ad format: print, electronic, digital, social, YouTube, SMS, e-mail, hoardings. — [RealtyNMore](https://realtynmore.com/promoters-must-publish-project-qr-codes-in-every-brochure-and-advertisement-up-rera/)
  - The two reports differ on whether allotment letters and BBAs are covered. Check the original UP RERA order.
- The UP RERA QR code resolves to the project's data on the UP RERA portal, including:
  - completion date and proposed start date
  - land documents
  - approved layouts and maps
  - specifications and amenities
  - bank accounts
  - promoters, co-promoters and registered agents
  - quarterly progress reports (QPRs)
  - occupation certificates and NOCs

  This is effectively the list of fields a platform can show or deep-link per project. — [RealtyNMore](https://realtynmore.com/promoters-must-publish-project-qr-codes-in-every-brochure-and-advertisement-up-rera/)
- **HARERA Gurugram vs Nitara Projects Ltd (2026).**
  - HARERA opened suo motu proceedings under Section 3(1), with Section 35 powers, against a promoter allegedly developing, advertising and selling residential floors without registration.
  - Its promotional material lacked the RERA registration number, the official HARERA website reference and the DTCP licence number.
  - Show-cause notice dated 16 Apr 2026. The promoter did not appear at the 11 May 2026 hearing. A final opportunity was set for 8 June 2026, with a warning of ex-parte proceedings.
  — [The Tribune, 13 May 2026](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/harera-cracks-down-on-gurugram-developer-over-unregistered-housing-project/); [Real Estate Law Journal](https://realestatelawjournal.in/harera-gurugram-initiates-suo-motu-action-for-alleged-rera-violations/)
- HARERA Gurugram also issued a public advisory. Buyers should check registration on the HARERA website before booking. Investing in an unregistered project is at the buyer's own "risk, cost and consequences" and may forfeit RERA remedies. — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/harera-cracks-down-on-gurugram-developer-over-unregistered-housing-project/); [Torbit Realty](https://torbitrealty.com/hrera-warns-homebuyers-against-booking-units-in-unregistered-projects/)
- Comparable MahaRERA enforcement (a signal of regulator practice, not NCR law):
  - Show-cause notices to 41 promoters for advertising without a registration number.
  - Penalties totalling ₹88.90 lakh on 628 projects that failed to show the RERA number and QR code in ads. The date of the article could not be confirmed.
  — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/maharera-issues-show-cause-notices-to-41-developers-for-advertising-projects-without-registration)

### Inferences
- **Hard listing gate for new-launch and under-construction inventory.** No listing should go live without:
  - a valid RERA registration number that is checked against the state portal, not just its format;
  - the authority's website URL;
  - for UP, the project QR code;
  - for Haryana, the DTCP licence number.
  Store registration validity dates and block listings whose registration has lapsed or been revoked.
- **Due-diligence features from public RERA data:** registration validity and extensions, QPR construction progress, complaints and orders against the promoter, and the list of registered agents for a project.

### Gaps
- No primary text of Delhi RERA, HARERA or UP RERA ad circulars was fetched. QR-code mandates for **Delhi RERA and HARERA** were not confirmed this session. Check the current circulars on rera.delhi.gov.in and haryanarera.gov.in.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Section 3 exemption.** Registration is not required where the land does not exceed 500 sq m or the project has no more than 8 apartments (states may lower these thresholds), or where a completion certificate was received before the Act commenced. Many Delhi builder floors and small Gurugram independent floors may fall under these thresholds. The Nitara case shows HARERA treats "residential floors" as registrable when the thresholds are crossed.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Key sections:**
  - Section 11(2): every ad or prospectus must show the authority's website address and the registration number.
  - Section 12: a promoter must compensate an allottee who suffers loss from a false advertisement or prospectus.
  - Section 10: agents must not facilitate sale of unregistered projects, must keep books of account, must not misrepresent, and must give allottees the documents they are entitled to.
  - Section 62: agent penalty of ₹10,000/day up to 5% of the cost of the unit whose sale was facilitated.
  - Sections 59–61: promoter penalties.
  - Section 31: complaints. Section 71: adjudicating officer for compensation. Section 43/44: Appellate Tribunal.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] HARERA has two authorities.** HARERA Gurugram covers Gurugram district. HARERA Panchkula covers the rest of Haryana, including Faridabad. UP RERA (Lucknow) has a regional office/bench for Gautam Buddh Nagar (Noida/Greater Noida). Delhi RERA covers the NCT.
- **2024–2026 Delhi RERA orders and UP RERA policy changes** (e.g., on QPR penalties, extensions, or Section 8 revival of stalled projects) could not be researched further this session.
- The exact current agent-registration fees and renewal rules for Delhi, Haryana and UP were not verified. State rules differ (e.g., individual vs company fees).

## 2. Key documents and approvals to verify (title chain, deeds, approvals, OC/CC, encumbrance, mutation, tax, society NOC, share certificate, possession, no-dues)

### Takeaway
The document set differs sharply by property type:
- DDA flat
- CGHS society flat
- Delhi builder floor
- Gurugram DTCP-licensed group housing
- Noida/Greater Noida authority leasehold flat

Encumbrance and dues checks run through state-specific channels: Haryana's ULB "No Dues Certificate" tied to a Property ID, UP's IGRSUP portal, and Delhi's sub-registrar/DORIS records. Haryana now ties registration to municipal no-dues and to a paperless workflow from 1 Nov 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **Gurugram:** tehsil offices were told not to register any property unless the file carries an NOC and a no-dues certificate from the Municipal Corporation for property tax and all pending dues. — [Realty+ (MCG)](https://www.rprealtyplus.com/news-views/no-registration-for-projects-with-pending-dues-mcg-78217.html)
- **Haryana NDC.** An urban local body (ULB) NDC certifies that no property tax or related municipal dues are outstanding against a specific Property ID as on the issue date. The Property ID is mandatory for paying property tax, updating ownership and downloading certificates on the NDC Haryana portal. Every due linked to the Property ID must be cleared, and the record must be current and self-certified. — [Haryana.com explainer](https://www.haryana.com/blog/property-tax-ndc-haryana-ulb-portal); [Zennest](https://zennestliving.com/ndc-haryana-portal/) (secondary)
- **Haryana paperless registration from 1 Nov 2025 across all 22 districts.**
  - Online guided sale-deed form, document upload, appointment slot and e-payment.
  - Physical appearance remains for biometrics and signing.
  — [100acress](https://www.100acress.com/blog/haryana-moves-to-paperless-land-registration-from-november-1) (secondary; check the official Haryana Revenue notification)
- **UP IGRSUP portal** is run by the Inspector-General of Registration and Stamps. It handles property registration, encumbrance certificates and stamp-duty calculation and payment. — [Bajaj Finserv via IGRS UP guide](https://translate.google.com/translate?u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.bajajfinserv.in%2Figrs-uttar-pradesh-guide&hl=hi&sl=en&tl=hi&client=srp); [Ujjivan SFB](https://www.ujjivansfb.bank.in/banking-blogs/personal-finance/uttar-pradesh-stamp-duty-registration-charges) (secondary)
- **Suraj Lamp.** An agreement to sell or a power of attorney does not transfer title. Only a registered conveyance (sale deed) does. Suraj Lamp & Industries v State of Haryana, decided 11 Oct 2011, (2012) 1 SCC 656. — [Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/1565619/)

### Inferences
Suggested baseline checklist by property type, built from the findings above and standard conveyancing practice. **[LAWYER REVIEW]**

- **Every resale flat:**
  - Registered sale/conveyance deed of the seller.
  - Chain of title deeds going back about 30 years where available (customary in practice, not a statutory period).
  - Mutation in municipal/authority records.
  - Latest property-tax receipts. In Haryana, the ULB NDC against the Property ID.
  - Society/RWA/maintenance no-dues.
  - Utility no-dues (electricity, water).
  - Bank NOC and original documents if mortgaged.
  - Encumbrance search: Delhi sub-registrar/DORIS; Haryana jamabandi/registration records; UP IGRSUP EC.
  - Identity/PAN of all owners.
  - Seller's capital-gains/TDS position.
- **Builder purchase (new or under-construction):**
  - RERA registration certificate.
  - Approved building plan.
  - Commencement Certificate.
  - DTCP licence (Haryana).
  - Allotment letter.
  - Builder-buyer agreement on the RERA model form.
  - Payment receipts.
  - For ready flats: Occupancy Certificate / Completion Certificate, possession letter and conveyance deed.
- **Noida / Greater Noida / YEIDA:**
  - Authority lease and sub-lease deed (tripartite).
  - Authority transfer permission/memorandum for resale.
  - Builder no-dues to the authority. This is critical given the land-dues crisis (see Q5).
- **Delhi CGHS:** share certificate, RCS/society NOC, freehold conversion deed or DDA lease.
- **DDA flats:** conveyance deed (freehold) or lease documents plus conversion status (see Q3).
- **Product design:** a structured "document vault" per listing with status flags (verified / missing / not applicable by property type) maps directly onto this list. Red-flag logic should trigger on:
  - GPA/SA/Will chains (Suraj Lamp)
  - missing OC
  - unregistered RERA project
  - pending authority dues (Noida)
  - a missing Haryana NDC

### Gaps
- **Delhi encumbrance.** How a buyer obtains an encumbrance certificate or search report in Delhi in 2026 could not be confirmed (sub-registrar inspection vs DORIS online search vs any newer NGDRS-based e-search). **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND]** Historically Delhi had no formal EC product. Lawyers run sub-registrar searches.
- **Haryana land records.** Whether jamabandi.nic.in (Web-HALRIS) exposes deed-level search for urban flats, versus only rural land jamabandi/mutation, was not verified.
- **IGRSUP coverage.** The years covered by IGRSUP online EC searches (often said to be about 2002 onwards) were not verified.
- **Paperless rollout.** No primary Haryana government notification on the paperless rollout and its legal effect was fetched.

## 3. Delhi-specific: DDA freehold conversion, CGHS, builder floors, unauthorized colonies / PM-UDAY, Lal Dora, GPA/SA/Will (Suraj Lamp), land pooling, MCD property tax, circle rates

### Takeaway
2025–2026 changes that matter:
- **DDA freehold conversion is frozen.** Leasehold-to-freehold conversion of residential properties is on hold pending a policy review (reported August 2026).
- **PM-UDAY widened.** On 7 April 2026 MoHUA cleared 1,511 of 1,731 unauthorised colonies for regularisation on an "as-is where-is" basis and dropped the layout-plan precondition. Applications run via the MCD SWAGAM portal from 24 April 2026.
- **Circle rates unchanged since 2014.** They are still those notified on 23 Sept 2014. A revision (possibly with a new "A+" category and hikes of up to about 35%) has been under review since June 2025 but was not notified as of mid-October 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **DDA freehold conversion freeze.**
  - Union MoS Tokhan Sahu stated the residential conversion policy is under review "to rationalise and simplify it so as to make it more citizen-friendly". "Pending such review, cases of conversion requests have been put on hold."
  - Covered stock: LIG/MIG/HIG and self-financing scheme flats, Asian Games Village flats, cooperative group housing society properties on DDA-leased land, and built-up residential plots.
  - No timeline was given for resumption.
  — [Business Today, 11 Aug 2026](https://www.businesstoday.in/real-estate/story/dda-puts-leasehold-to-freehold-conversions-on-hold-which-delhi-homes-are-covered-under-the-policy-548432-2026-08-11); [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/delhi-development-authority-puts-freehold-conversions-on-hold/)
- **Earlier conversion rules (pre-freeze).**
  - Conversion was allowed only where there was no title dispute and land use was residential.
  - NOCs from all mortgagees were required.
  - Charges could be paid in instalments over up to 5 years at 12% interest.
  — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/real-estate/story/dda-puts-leasehold-to-freehold-conversions-on-hold-which-delhi-homes-are-covered-under-the-policy-548432-2026-08-11)
- **PM-UDAY regularisation (7 Apr 2026).**
  - MoHUA cleared 1,511 of 1,731 colonies, benefiting about 45 lakh residents.
  - 220 colonies are excluded: forests, Yamuna floodplain (Zone-O), ridge, monument-protected areas, and 69 affluent colonies.
  - The layout-plan approval requirement was dispensed with, and land use is treated as residential. Residents can now seek building-plan approval.
  - The decision operates under the National Capital Territory of Delhi (Recognition of Property Rights of Residents in Unauthorised Colonies) Regulations, 2019.
  - Applications open on MCD SWAGAM from 24 Apr 2026.
  - About 40,000 conveyance deeds/authorisation slips had been issued from 2019 to 31 Mar 2026.
  — [PIB Tracker, 7 Apr 2026](https://pibtracker.com/article/2026-04-07/delhi-colony-regularisation-2249686); [India.com](https://www.india.com/news/india/pm-uday-scheme-clears-1511-unauthorised-colonies-45-lakh-delhi-residents-to-get-ownership-rights-says-cm-rekha-gupta-8371739/)
- **PM-UDAY application deadline.** One secondary source states applications must be submitted by **31 Oct 2026**. This was not confirmed against an official notice. — [Vajiram & Ravi](https://vajiramandravi.com/current-affairs/pm-uday-yojana/)
- **Delhi circle rates.**
  - The rates in force were notified on 23 Sept 2014: Category A ₹7,74,000/sq m down to Category H ₹23,280/sq m.
  - In June 2025, CM Rekha Gupta announced a review committee, with a possible A+ category and increases of up to 35%.
  - No revised notification had issued as of about 15 Oct 2026.
  — [99acres](https://www.99acres.com/articles/circle-rates-in-delhi.html); [DelhiPropertyRegistration](https://www.delhipropertyregistration.com/circle-rate-delhi) (secondary; check the Delhi Revenue Department site)
- **Delhi stamp duty:** 6% (men), 4% (women), 5% (joint man+woman). Registration fee is 1% plus ₹100 pasting fee. Duty is computed on the higher of transaction value or circle rate. — [HomeFirst](https://homefirstindia.com/blog/article/stamp-duty-and-registration-charges-in-delhi); [Bajaj Finserv Markets](https://www.bajajfinservmarkets.in/resources/stamp-duty/stamp-duty-in-delhi) (secondary)
- **Delhi land pooling (MPD-2041 framework).**
  - DDA says 104 villages form the Development Area where the policy applies (105 villages notified as Urban Area), across six zones.
  - In November 2025, Sector 8B of Zone P-II (Gadi Khasro/Ibrahimpur) became the first sector to achieve 70% contiguous pooled land and become eligible for development.
  - DDA ran village outreach camps in Oct 2026 (Bakkarwala, Tigipur, Sultanpur Dabas). The policy is pitched as enabling about 12 lakh homes.
  — [TogetherBuying](https://togetherbuying.in/articles/delhi-urban-extension-village-list-2026-which-104-villages-come-under-dda-land-pooling); [The News Mill, Oct 2026](https://thenewsmill.com/2026/10/dda-holds-outreach-camps-to-promote-land-pooling-policy-in-delhi/); [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/delhi/dda-drive-to-take-land-pooling-policy-to-villages)
- **GPA/SA/Will sales.** In Suraj Lamp (11 Oct 2011) the Supreme Court held that SA/GPA/Will transactions do not convey title. Only a registered deed of conveyance does. — [Indian Kanoon](https://indiankanoon.org/doc/1565619/)

### Inferences
- **DDA freeze.** Listings of DDA/CGHS leasehold flats in 2026 cannot assume a buyer can convert to freehold soon. The platform should flag "leasehold – conversion currently suspended" and ask whether the seller already holds a freehold conveyance deed.
- **PM-UDAY.** Unauthorised-colony properties need a distinct risk tier: PM-UDAY conveyance deed/authorisation slip present vs applied vs none. Only properties with a PM-UDAY conveyance deed or authorisation slip should be eligible for "verified title" badges.
- **Circle-rate revision.** A Delhi revision, if notified, will raise stamp duty and the Section 50C/56(2)(x) benchmarks overnight. Keep circle rates as versioned data with effective dates.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Builder floors.** MPD-2021 amendments (2018) allowed stilt parking plus 4 floors with higher FAR on residential plots. Many builder floors lack a full Occupancy/Completion Certificate, or have deviations (extra floor, stilt enclosure). Buyers should verify the sanctioned plan and the OC/CC from MCD's building department. No 2025–2026 MCD/DDA rule change on builder-floor OCs was verified.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Delhi stamp duty composition.** Whether the 6% includes the 1% MCD transfer duty was not confirmed.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] CGHS.** CGHS flats in Delhi are governed by the Delhi Cooperative Societies Act 2003 and Rules 2007. Transfer requires society/RCS membership processes, and the share certificate evidences membership.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Lal Dora / extended Lal Dora.** These areas lack conventional revenue records and building-plan regimes. Title is usually established by possession and village records, and SVAMITVA property cards are being rolled out. Lenders often refuse financing. Current 2025–2026 Delhi policy on Lal Dora registration and building-plan approval was not verified.
- **MCD property tax.** The current regime (unit-area method, online payment, any 2025–26 amnesty scheme) was not researched in this session.
- **Delhi registration system.** Whether Delhi moved registration to NGDRS or another new e-registration system in 2024–2026 was not verified.

## 4. Haryana (Gurugram / Faridabad): stamp duty, collector rates, registration, NDC, jamabandi, DTCP licences, EDC/IDC

### Takeaway
Gurugram urban stamp duty is 7% (men), 5% (women) and 6% (joint). Haryana revised collector rates twice in eight months: 1 Dec 2024 and 1 Aug 2025. The August 2025 round lifted some old-Gurugram residential sector rates by about 77%. Since 1 Nov 2025 registration has moved to a paperless workflow, and Gurugram registrations require municipal NOC/no-dues.

### Cited Findings
- **Stamp duty (urban):** men 7%, women 5%, joint man+woman 6%. **Rural:** men 5%, women 3%. — [RupayWise](https://rupaywise.com/calculators/stamp-duty/haryana); [ghar.tv](https://www.ghar.tv/blog/gurugram-collector-rates-2025-26-complete-guide-to-stamp-duty-property-registration/artid4702) (secondary). ThePrint also cites 7% for men and 5% for women. — [ThePrint, 1 Aug 2025](https://theprint.in/india/sudden-hike-in-haryana-circle-rates-is-2nd-since-dec-stamp-duty-as-high-as-145-in-parts-of-gurugram/2709912/)
- **Collector (circle) rate revisions.**
  - Revised effective 1 Dec 2024 and again effective 1 Aug 2025.
  - Old-Gurugram residential sectors rose 77%, from ₹25,300 to ₹45,000 per sq yd. DLF colony rose from ₹79,300 to ₹95,000 per sq yd.
  - Agricultural land rose up to 145% (Bajghera) and 108% (Sarhaul).
  - The Financial Commissioner's letter said the revised rates "may be enforced at the earliest preferably from 1 August, 2025 as it captures only a fraction of the increased transaction value."
  — [ThePrint, 1 Aug 2025](https://theprint.in/india/sudden-hike-in-haryana-circle-rates-is-2nd-since-dec-stamp-duty-as-high-as-145-in-parts-of-gurugram/2709912/)
- **Registration fee (conflicting sources).**
  - One source: 1% of the higher of circle rate or sale value, minimum ₹1,000.
  - Another source: under the new paperless system, the fee is simplified to ₹2,000 per transaction (urban) and ₹1,000 (rural, plus ₹500 per extra acre).
  — [100acress](https://www.100acress.com/blog/haryana-moves-to-paperless-land-registration-from-november-1); [Godrej Capital](https://www.godrejcapital.com/media-blog/knowledge-centre/stamp-duty-and-registration-charges-haryana); [NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/blog/stamp-duty-and-property-registration-charges-in-haryana/) (secondary, conflicting)
- **Paperless registration** in all 22 districts from 1 Nov 2025. — [100acress](https://www.100acress.com/blog/haryana-moves-to-paperless-land-registration-from-november-1)
- **Municipal NOC + no-dues prerequisite** for registration in Gurugram (MCG direction to tehsils). — [Realty+](https://www.rprealtyplus.com/news-views/no-registration-for-projects-with-pending-dues-mcg-78217.html)
- **DTCP licence number in ads.** HARERA treats a missing DTCP licence number in ads as a compliance failure (Nitara, 2026). — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/harera-cracks-down-on-gurugram-developer-over-unregistered-housing-project/)

### Inferences
- **Calculator.** Haryana stamp-duty and fee calculators must:
  - apply gender and urban/rural rates;
  - use the collector-rate version effective on the registration date (1 Dec 2024 and 1 Aug 2025 versions differ);
  - compute duty on the higher of consideration or collector rate.
- **Seller blockers.** Show the Property ID and NDC status as pre-registration blockers for sellers.

### Gaps
- **Registration-fee schedule.** The definitive post-Nov-2025 Haryana fee schedule could not be verified. Sources conflict between a 1% fee and flat/slab fees. **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND]** Haryana historically used a slab-based fee schedule capped at about ₹50,000. Confirm on the Haryana Revenue/IGR site.
- **2026-27 collector rates.** Whether Haryana issued a new collector-rate revision in 2026 was not verified.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] DTCP licensing and EDC/IDC.**
  - Group housing colonies in Gurugram are licensed by DTCP under the Haryana Development and Regulation of Urban Areas Act, 1975.
  - Developers pay External and Internal Development Charges (EDC/IDC) to the state. Unpaid EDC can block OC/registration and is sometimes passed to buyers.
  - Section 7-A of the 1975 Act requires a DTCP NOC to register deeds of vacant land below a size threshold in controlled/urbanisable areas.
  - Flats in Haryana are governed by the Haryana Apartment Ownership Act, 1983, which requires a Deed of Declaration.
- **e-stamping.** Haryana's mechanism (e-GRAS vs SHCIL) was not verified.
- **Jamabandi coverage.** What jamabandi.nic.in exposes for urban flats was not verified (see Q2).

## 5. Uttar Pradesh (Noida / Greater Noida / Ghaziabad): stamp duty, women concession, circle rates, IGRSUP, authority leasehold model, tripartite agreements, registry backlog and the Amitabh Kant committee

### Takeaway
- **Stamp duty:** UP charges 7% (men). Since 23 July 2025, women get a 1% concession on property up to ₹1 crore (maximum benefit about ₹1 lakh). Earlier the concession applied only up to ₹10 lakh value, with a ₹10,000 cap.
- **Circle rates:** Gautam Buddh Nagar proposed hikes in 2025. Ghaziabad shelved a proposed 40% hike for 2025–26.
- **Registry backlog:** Noida/Greater Noida remains defined by it. The UP government accepted the Amitabh Kant committee package in December 2023, under which registries unlock once builders pay 25% of land dues. Implementation has been partial. The latest reliable figures found are from March 2024.

### Cited Findings
- **Women's concession.** The UP Cabinet (23 July 2025) approved a 1% stamp-duty discount on property worth up to ₹1 crore bought in a woman's name. It replaced the earlier concession that applied only up to ₹10 lakh value, with a maximum rebate of ₹10,000. — [News On AIR, 23 Jul 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/up-cabinet-approves-1-stamp-duty-exemption-for-women-on-property-worth-up-to-1-crore-rupees)
- **Noida rates.** Stamp duty is 7% for men and 6% for a sole female owner, and the registration fee is 1%. — [Square Yards](https://www.squareyards.com/blog/stamp-duty-and-registration-charges-in-noida) (secondary)
- **Conflicting claims on UP extras:**

## 7. Taxes: GST, TDS, capital gains, stamp-duty-value deeming, home-loan deductions, and the Income-tax Act 2025 renumbering

### Takeaway
- **GST:** unchanged by the September 2025 "GST 2.0" reform. It is 1% for affordable housing and 5% for other under-construction units, both without ITC, and nil on completed units with OC/CC.
- **New Income-tax Act:** the Income-tax Act 2025 replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026, with a single "tax year" concept. Rates and thresholds for property were broadly carried over, but section numbers changed:
  - 194-IA → 393(1)
  - 54 → 82; 54EC → 85; 54F → 86
  - 50C → 78; 43CA → 53; 56(2)(x) → 92(2)(m)
  - 24(b) → 20
  - 80C → 123; 80EEA → 131
  - 269SS → 185; 269ST → 186; 269T → 188
- **LTCG on property:** 12.5% without indexation (since 23 July 2024). Resident individuals/HUFs can opt for 20% with indexation on property acquired before 23 July 2024. The long-term holding period is 24 months.

### Cited Findings
- **GST.**
  - GST 2.0 (effective 22 Sept 2025) kept real-estate rates unchanged: 1% affordable and 5% other residential, without ITC.
  - Affordable housing means value up to ₹45 lakh and carpet area up to 60 sq m in metros or 90 sq m in non-metros.
  - Completed flats with a completion/occupancy certificate attract no GST.
  - Cement GST was cut from 28% to 18%.
  — [TaxClue](https://taxclue.in/gst-on-flat-purchase); [HomeFirst](https://homefirstindia.com/blog/article/gst-on-flat-purchase-in-india) (secondary)
- **Income-tax Act 2025.**
  - Passed by the Lok Sabha on 11 Aug 2025 and the Rajya Sabha on 12 Aug 2025. Presidential assent on 21 Aug 2025. Effective 1 Apr 2026.
  - 536 sections, 23 chapters, 16 schedules. A unified "Tax Year" replaces previous year/assessment year.
  - Draft rules and forms were released for comment on 7 Feb 2026.
  — [Wikipedia: Income-tax Act, 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Income-tax_Act,_2025)
- **TDS on property.**
  - Old 194-IA is now Section 393 (393(1) for resident sellers) from 1 Apr 2026.
  - 1% TDS where consideration is ₹50 lakh or more, computed on the higher of consideration or stamp-duty value. 20% if the seller has no PAN.
  - Agricultural land is excluded.
  — [TDSGuru](https://tdsguru.in/sections/194IA.html); [Olambit](https://www.olambit.com/blog/tds-property-purchase-194ia-new-act-2025/)
- **TDS forms (sources conflict).**
  - One source says Form 26QB is replaced by Form 141 (Schedule B) and Form 16B by Form 132. — search summary of [LegalSuvidha](https://legalsuvidha.com/blog/tds-on-property-purchase-194ia)
  - Another still refers to Form 26QB / 16B. — [Olambit](https://www.olambit.com/blog/tds-property-purchase-194ia-new-act-2025/)
  - TDSGuru refers to a "challan-cum-statement" and does not name the form.
  - Unresolved. Check the incometax.gov.in e-filing portal.
- **TDS on NRI sellers.**
  - Old 195 regime applies, needing a TAN. One source maps it to "Section 400" and quotes about 20% on LTCG, which is outdated after the July 2024 rate cut to 12.5%. — [Olambit](https://www.olambit.com/blog/tds-property-purchase-194ia-new-act-2025/)

  - Both are unverified. **[LAWYER REVIEW]**
- **Capital gains.**
  - Immovable property is long-term after 24 months.
  - Property acquired on or after 23 July 2024: 12.5% without indexation only.
  - Property acquired before 23 July 2024 and sold on or after that date: resident individuals and HUFs pay the lower of 12.5% without indexation or 20% with indexation. This option is for immovable property only.
  — [FinCalculator](https://fincalculator.in/blog/property-capital-gains-tax-indexation-vs-12-5-guide); [StudioMatrx](https://www.studiomatrx.org/guides/capital-gains-india-2026); [TaxGuru (new Act capital gains)](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/capital-gains-income-tax-act-2025-tax-period-2026-27.html)

  - 54→82, 54EC→85, 54F→86, 80C→123, 80EEA→131, 48→72, 139A→262. — [ClearTax mapping](https://cleartax.in/s/income-tax-act-2025-section-numbers-old-vs-new)

  - 50C→78, 43CA→53, 56(2)(x)→92(2)(m). — search summary of [TaxGuru](https://taxguru.in/income-tax/section-50c-43ca-56-2-stamp-duty-taxation.html) / [Coraa](https://coraa.ai/blog/stamp-duty-safe-harbour-43ca-50c-56-2-x)

### Inferences
- **Show both section numbers.** Product copy and calculators should show both the old (1961) and new (2025) section numbers through at least FY 2026-27. Users, banks and CAs will mix them.
- **Seller tax estimator** should:
  - compute both grandfathering options for pre-23-Jul-2024 acquisitions;
  - flag when the agreed price is below 90.9% of circle value (the 110% test), which triggers 50C/78 deeming for the seller and 56(2)(x)/92(2)(m) income for the buyer.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] TDS base and threshold.**
  - Since 1 Sep 2019 the TDS base includes club membership, car parking, electricity/water facility fees, maintenance and advance fees and similar charges.
  - From 1 Oct 2024 (Finance (No. 2) Act 2024) the ₹50 lakh threshold is tested on total consideration even where there are multiple buyers or sellers.
  - TDS must be deposited within 30 days from the end of the month of deduction.
  - Confirm whether these were carried into Section 393 unchanged.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Exemptions.**
  - 54/54F: exemption capped at ₹10 crore from AY 2024-25.
  - 54EC: up to ₹50 lakh in specified bonds within 6 months, 5-year lock-in.
  - 56(2)(x): buyer taxable if SDV minus consideration exceeds the higher of ₹50,000 or 10% of consideration.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Home-loan deductions.**
  - Old regime: 24(b) interest up to ₹2 lakh for a self-occupied property, and 80C principal plus stamp duty within ₹1.5 lakh.
  - New regime (default since FY 2023-24): no 24(b) deduction for self-occupied property. Interest on let-out property is deductible, but house-property loss cannot be set off against other heads or carried forward.
  - 80EEA (₹1.5 lakh extra) applied only to loans sanctioned 1 Apr 2019 – 31 Mar 2022 for houses with stamp value ≤ ₹45 lakh. It is not available to new borrowers.
  - Whether the 2025 Act changed any of this was not verified.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] GST on platform fees.** Brokerage/commission or platform fees attract 18% GST. Sale of a completed building (after CC/OC) is outside GST (Schedule III).
- **Budget 2026 (1 Feb 2026).** Any property-specific changes were not researched beyond the unconfirmed "PAN-based NRI TDS" mention.

## 9. Benami, PMLA / FIU-IND obligations for real estate agents, cash limits

### Takeaway
Since 28 Dec 2020, real estate agents are "persons carrying on designated business or profession" under the PMLA. Agents whose turnover is ₹20 lakh or more must register with FIU-IND (FINGate 2.0), appoint a Principal Officer, do customer due diligence (CDD), keep records for 5 years, and file STRs and CTRs. A monetised platform that facilitates deals may fall in this category. Cash receipts of ₹2 lakh or more (s.186, ex-269ST) and cash advances for property of ₹20,000 or more (s.185, ex-269SS) are prohibited.

### Cited Findings
- **Designation and definition.**
  - Notification G.S.R. 798(E) of 28 Dec 2020 designated real estate agents under Section 2(1)(sa)(iii) PMLA.
  - G.S.R. 855(E) of 29 Nov 2022 refined the definition, linked to RERA, to cover persons facilitating sale or purchase with annual turnover of ₹20 lakh or more.
  - The threshold is the agent's own revenue, not the property value.
  — [Compliance7 blog, Apr 2026](https://blog.compliance7.com/2026/04/22/fiu-ind-registration-real-estate-agents-india/) (secondary; notification numbers should be checked on egazette.gov.in)
- **Reports and duties.**
  - Agents file STRs, CTRs (cash over ₹10 lakh in a month) and Counterfeit Currency Reports on FINGate 2.0.
  - Agents must register (FIUREID), appoint a Principal Officer, perform CDD and keep records for 5 years.
  — [AMLIndia](https://amlindia.in/entities-subject-to-fiu-ind-reporting-on-fingate-2-portal/); [Compliance7](https://blog.compliance7.com/2026/04/22/fiu-ind-registration-real-estate-agents-india/)
- **Registrar reporting.** Property transaction reports for ₹50 lakh+ properties are filed by sub-registrars, not agents. Agents should apply enhanced due diligence and file STRs on red flags. — [Compliance7](https://blog.compliance7.com/2026/04/22/fiu-ind-registration-real-estate-agents-india/)
- **Cash limits:** s.185 (ex-269SS) and s.186 (ex-269ST), as in Q7. — [Coraa](https://coraa.ai/university/cash-transaction-compliance-checker)

### Inferences
- **FIU-IND registration.** If the platform earns brokerage or success fees and crosses ₹20 lakh turnover, plan for FIU-IND registration, a KYC/CDD layer for transacting parties, and STR workflows. **[LAWYER REVIEW]** is needed on whether a pure listing/ads model is captured.
- **Cash.** Block or flag any in-app "cash component" negotiation features. Cash components in resale deals are a known market risk (not quantified in this research) and are illegal above these limits.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Benami law.** The Benami Transactions (Prohibition) Amendment Act 2016 is in force. In Union of India v Ganpati Dealcom (Aug 2022) the Supreme Court struck down parts of it, including retrospective application. In Oct 2024 the Court recalled that judgment on review, so the issue is pending re-hearing. Current status not verified.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] SFT reporting.** Sub-registrars report property transactions of ₹30 lakh or more to the Income-tax Department via SFT (old s.285BA, Form 61A). The new-Act section number was not found.

## 10. Platform-level: does an online listing platform need RERA agent registration in Delhi/Haryana/UP? Regulator actions against portals

### Takeaway
RERA's definition of "real estate agent" (s.2(zm)) covers anyone who, for remuneration, negotiates or acts for a party, or introduces prospective buyers and sellers through "any medium". MahaRERA invoked this against Magicbricks, 99acres, Makaan and Housing.com in 2018. No NCR regulator order specifically directed at portals was found. A platform that charges commission, success fees or lead fees tied to RERA-registered projects is at real risk of being treated as an agent in each state. Registering with Delhi RERA, HARERA (Gurugram and Panchkula) and UP RERA is low-cost insurance.

### Cited Findings
- **MahaRERA notices to portals (8 Aug 2018).**
  - Notices under Section 9 to Magicbricks, 99acres, Makaan and Housing.com, asking them to prove registration or explain why they had not registered.
  - The portals argued they were not profit-making intermediaries and collected no commissions.
  - Consumer advocates relied on s.2(zm), which covers anyone who "introduces, prospective buyers and sellers to each other" through "any medium" for remuneration.
  - The portals sought time; the next hearing was set for 29 Aug 2018.
  — [Moneylife, 8 Aug 2018](https://www.moneylife.in/article/maharera-issues-notices-to-magicbricks-99acres-makaan-and-housingcom/54960.html)
- **Section 9 penalty.** Facilitating sale in a registered project without agent registration attracts ₹10,000 per day, up to 5% of cost. — [LegalServiceIndia](https://www.legalserviceindia.com/Legal-Articles/rera-agent-registration-india-guide/)
- **NCR regulator focus.** HARERA Gurugram's 2026 enforcement focuses on promoters' unregistered marketing and missing RERA/DTCP details in ads, not on portals. The same compliance logic applies to anyone publishing such ads. — [The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/harera-cracks-down-on-gurugram-developer-over-unregistered-housing-project/)

### Inferences
- **Lower-risk model.** Pure classifieds with no transaction-linked remuneration, where sellers list their own completed flats, is outside most RERA hooks. Owner-to-owner resale of completed units is generally not a "real estate project" sale.
- **Higher-risk model.** Brokerage/commission, "assured" lead fees from builders, site-visit facilitation or negotiation for new projects. Treat this as requiring agent registration in each state and compliance with s.10 agent duties: no unregistered projects, books of account, no misrepresentation.
- **Listing obligations either way.**
  - Do not list unregistered RERA projects.
  - Show the RERA number, authority website, QR code (UP) and DTCP licence (Haryana) on project pages.
  - Keep audit logs of who supplied ad content.

### Gaps
- The outcome of the 2018 MahaRERA–portal proceedings was not found.
- Whether any NCR authority (Delhi RERA, HARERA, UP RERA) has issued a circular or order to listing portals specifically was not found.
- **[LAWYER REVIEW]** Whether a lead-gen/AI-matching model constitutes "facilitation" under s.9 in each state.

## 11. Platform-level: liability for fake listings; Consumer Protection Act 2019 and E-Commerce Rules 2020; IT Act intermediary rules (incl. 2026 amendment); CCPA dark-patterns guidelines

### Takeaway
The platform will likely be treated as an "e-commerce entity" under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020 and as an "intermediary" under the IT Act / IT Rules 2021. Key dated obligations:
- **Dark patterns:** the CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns (30 Nov 2023) list 13 prohibited patterns. A CCPA advisory of 5 June 2025 required all e-commerce platforms to self-audit within 3 months and declare compliance. 26 platforms had declared compliance by late 2025.
- **IT Rules amendment (notified 10 Feb 2026, effective 20 Feb 2026):**
  - takedown on court/government orders within 3 hours (previously 36);
  - user grievances resolved within 7 days (previously 15);
  - new synthetic-content duties (see Q13).

### Cited Findings
- **Dark-pattern rules.**
  - CCPA issued the Dark Patterns Guidelines on 30 Nov 2023 (13 patterns).
  - The 5 June 2025 advisory mandated self-audits within 3 months.
  - In 2025, 26 platforms declared compliance.
  — [LearnThruPractice (PIB digest)](https://www.learnthrupractice.com/upsc-pib-item/2308759/government-amends-e-commerce-rules-strengthen-consumer-protection); [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/consumer-protection/ccpa's-crackdown-on-dark-patterns-mandates-self-audit-to-eliminate-dark-patterns-in-3-months); [PIB: 26 platforms declare compliance](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2191948&reg=3&lang=2)
- **Explicit consent.** The advisory cited Rule 4(9) of the E-Commerce Rules: consent must be explicit and affirmative, with no pre-ticked boxes. — [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/consumer-protection/ccpa's-crackdown-on-dark-patterns-mandates-self-audit-to-eliminate-dark-patterns-in-3-months); [Chambers](https://chambers.com/articles/central-consumer-protection-authority-advisory-on-self-audit)
- **2021 draft E-Commerce amendments.** Draft amendments (Chief Compliance Officer, nodal officer, Resident Grievance Officer, curbs on flash sales) were released in June 2021. Their final notification status is unclear. — [LearnThruPractice](https://www.learnthrupractice.com/upsc-pib-item/2308759/government-amends-e-commerce-rules-strengthen-consumer-protection)
- **IT Rules Amendment 2026.**
  - Notified 10 Feb 2026, effective 20 Feb 2026.
  - Takedown on court or government notices cut from 36 hours to 3 hours. Certain harmful content: 36 hours. Intimate/impersonation content: 2 hours.
  - Grievance resolution cut from 15 days to 7 days (Rule 3(1)(c)).
  — [Chandhiok & Mahajan](https://chandhiok.com/insights/cm-e-alert-ministry-of-electronics-and-information-technology-notifies-the-information-technology-intermediary-guidelines-and-digital-media-ethics-code-amendment-rules-2026); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/02/223-meity-amendments-it-rules-synthetic-media-deepfakes/); [HLC](https://www.hlc.com/en/publications/india-introduces-mandatory-labelling-for-ai-and-3hour-takedown-for-illegal-content)

### Inferences
- **Fake listings.** Safe harbour under s.79 IT Act depends on due diligence. Recommended controls:
  - seller KYC;
  - ownership-document check before a "verified" badge;
  - a grievance officer with 7-day resolution;
  - a 3-hour takedown capability for government or court orders;
  - repeat-offender bans.

  "Verified" badges create consumer-law exposure if verification is shallow. Define precisely what was verified.
- **Dark-pattern audit before launch.** Check fake urgency ("3 people viewing"), basket sneaking, drip pricing (hidden platform or legal fees) and forced-continuity subscriptions for broker plans.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Consumer Protection Act 2019 and E-Commerce Rules 2020.**
  - CPA 2019, s.2(28) defines misleading advertisement. CCPA can impose penalties of up to ₹10 lakh (₹50 lakh for repeat offences) and act against endorsers. Note that s.2(28) and the penalty powers sit in separate provisions of the Act.
  - The CCPA Guidelines on Misleading Advertisements and Endorsements are dated 9 June 2022.
  - The E-Commerce Rules (notified 23 July 2020) require marketplace entities to:
    - appoint a grievance officer (acknowledge within 48 hours, resolve within 1 month);
    - display seller details;
    - not manipulate prices.
- **Fake-review standard.** BIS IS 19000:2022 on online consumer reviews was not verified.
- **Portal case law.** No consumer-commission or court decision holding a property portal liable for a fake listing was found this session.
- **2026 CCPA actions.** A 2026 PIB release titled "CCPA Acts Against Dark Patterns on Digital Platforms" exists (PRID 2268302) but could not be fetched (403). Its content is unknown.

## 12. Platform-level: Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025

### Takeaway
The DPDP Rules were notified on 14 November 2025 with an 18-month phase-in:
- **Immediately:** Data Protection Board machinery.
- **12 months (around Nov 2026):** consent-manager provisions.
- **13 May 2027:** full substantive obligations, including notice, consent, rights, security, breach reporting and retention.

For a property platform, the core issues are:
- purpose-specific consent before sharing buyer or seller leads with brokers, builders or lenders;
- breach notice to the Board within 72 hours;
- security logs kept for 1 year;
- retention and erasure schedules;
- penalties of up to ₹250 crore.

### Cited Findings
- **Notification and phase-in.**
  - Final DPDP Rules notified 14 Nov 2025.
  - Board provisions immediate. Consent Manager provisions after 12 months (Nov 2026). Full compliance by 13 May 2027.
  — [Seqrite](https://www.seqrite.com/blog/dpdp-rules-are-here-what-changed-from-the-draft/); [DPDPActIndia timeline](https://www.dpdpactindia.com/timeline.html); [PIB announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2190014&reg=3&lang=2) (PIB page not fetchable, 403)
- **Consent managers** must be Indian-incorporated companies with net worth of at least ₹2 crore. — search summary of [DPDPActIndia](https://www.dpdpactindia.com/timeline.html) / [TCSA](https://www.tcsa.in/resources/dpdp-rules-2025-implementation-roadmap)
- **Key rules (secondary summary).**
  - Rule 3: clear notice, with vernacular options.
  - Rule 6: security safeguards — encryption, access control, logging, backups, 1-year log retention, vendor contracts.
  - Rule 7: breach intimation to affected users and to the Board within 72 hours.
  - Third Schedule: 3-year retention/erasure for large e-commerce, social-media and gaming platforms, with a 48-hour pre-erasure notice.
  - Rule 10: verifiable parental consent for under-18s.
  - Significant Data Fiduciaries: annual audit and algorithmic due diligence.
  - Penalties: up to ₹250 crore (security failure), ₹200 crore (breach notice or children's data), ₹150 crore (SDF), ₹50 crore (residual).
  — [Consent.in](https://www.consent.in/blog/dpdp-rules) (secondary; check against the gazette text)

### Inferences
- **Consent for lead sharing.** Buyer enquiries shared with sellers, brokers, builders, or lenders/DSAs need a specific, itemised consent at the point of capture, revocable at any time. Each recipient is either a processor under contract or an independent fiduciary. Avoid bundled "agree to be contacted by partners" checkboxes, which also fall foul of E-Commerce Rule 4(9) and the dark-pattern rules.
- **Data map.** Build a data inventory of PAN/Aadhaar copies, title documents (which contain third-party personal data), call recordings and WhatsApp chats. Also build a 72-hour breach runbook and a retention schedule before May 2027.
- **SDF designation.** If the platform scales, the government may notify it as a Significant Data Fiduciary, which brings DPO, audit and DPIA duties.

### Gaps
- The gazette text of the Rules was not read this session.
- **Third Schedule thresholds.** Whether they (e.g., e-commerce entities with ≥2 crore registered users) would cover an NCR-only property platform was not verified.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Act basics.** The DPDP Act received assent on 11 Aug 2023. s.3(c)(ii) excludes personal data made publicly available by the data principal or by a person under a legal obligation. This matters for registration and RERA data; see Q14.

## 13. Platform-level: AI-generated / virtually staged images in property advertising

### Takeaway
There is no real-estate-specific AI-imagery rule in India as of Oct 2026. Three regimes overlap:
- **IT Rules amendment of 10 Feb 2026 (effective 20 Feb 2026).** It regulates "synthetically generated information" (SGI). Intermediaries offering AI-generation tools must label the output prominently and embed permanent metadata or identifiers. Routine edits such as brightness, noise reduction and compression are excluded.
- **RERA s.12.** A promoter is liable to compensate for loss caused by false ads.
- **Consumer Protection Act / CCPA and ASCI.** These cover misleading-advertising rules.

Virtual staging of a real flat is probably SGI if it shows non-existent furniture or finishes as real. Label it.

### Cited Findings
- **SGI definition.** "audio, visual or audio-visual information which is artificially or algorithmically created, generated, modified or altered" so that it appears real or authentic, depicting individuals or events "indistinguishable from a natural person or real-world event". — [Chandhiok & Mahajan](https://chandhiok.com/insights/cm-e-alert-ministry-of-electronics-and-information-technology-notifies-the-information-technology-intermediary-guidelines-and-digital-media-ethics-code-amendment-rules-2026)
- **Exclusions (Rule 2(1)(wa)):**
  - routine editing (brightness, noise reduction, compression);
  - good-faith document creation (presentations, templates);
  - accessibility improvements.
  — [Chandhiok & Mahajan](https://chandhiok.com/insights/cm-e-alert-ministry-of-electronics-and-information-technology-notifies-the-information-technology-intermediary-guidelines-and-digital-media-ethics-code-amendment-rules-2026); [MediaNama](https://www.medianama.com/2026/02/223-meity-amendments-it-rules-synthetic-media-deepfakes/)
- **Duties of intermediaries offering SGI creation tools:**
  - deploy measures against unlawful SGI;
  - make sure SGI carries a "prominent" label;
  - embed permanent metadata or unique identifiers;
  - prevent removal of labels and metadata.

  Significant social media intermediaries must also obtain user declarations and verify them. — [Chandhiok & Mahajan](https://chandhiok.com/insights/cm-e-alert-ministry-of-electronics-and-information-technology-notifies-the-information-technology-intermediary-guidelines-and-digital-media-ethics-code-amendment-rules-2026); [Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/media-telecoms-it-entertainment/2026-it-rules-amendments-ai-content-labelling-and-intermediary-obligations)
- **Commentary on scope.** Some commentary reads the SGI definition as aimed at deepfakes of real persons and events. — [Veritect](https://veritect.ai/digital-data-ai-law/it-rules-synthetic-media-amendment-feb-2026); [Internet Freedom Foundation critique](https://internetfreedom.in/it-intermediary-amendment-rules-2026-contradict-their-purpose/)
- **ASCI code.** Chapter I requires that factual claims be "capable of substantiation". No AI- or real-estate-specific clause was visible on the code page fetched. — [ASCI Code](https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code/)
- **UP RERA's rationale.** The QR mandate was explicitly aimed at "reducing the chances of misinformation or deceptive advertising practices". — [Construction World](https://www.constructionworld.in/latest-construction-news/real-estate-news/up-rera-mandates-qr-codes-for-projects/59405/)

### Inferences
- **Recommended policy for virtual staging and AI renders:**
  - a persistent on-image label ("Virtually staged" / "AI-generated render");
  - C2PA-style or other embedded metadata;
  - the original unstaged photo shown alongside;
  - no AI alteration of structural features (views, room size, windows, damp or cracks);
  - for under-construction projects, artist's-impression disclaimers that match RERA-approved plans.

  This covers IT Rules 2026, CPA misleading-ad risk and RERA s.12 compensation exposure.
- **Whether the rules bite.** It is debatable whether a staged flat photo "depicts a real-world event" within the SGI definition. Labelling anyway is the prudent course. **[LAWYER REVIEW]**

### Gaps
- **ASCI guidance.** No ASCI guideline specifically on real-estate ads or AI/digitally altered images was confirmed. An ASCI real-estate guideline is believed to exist (it requires artist's-impression disclaimers and RERA numbers) but was not fetched. **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND]**
- **Regulator orders.** No NCR RERA order on AI renders or virtual staging was found.

## 14. Platform-level: publishing transaction/price data scraped from registration portals; legality of accessing government land records

### Takeaway
No authoritative 2024–2026 source was found on the legality of scraping IGRSUP, DORIS, jamabandi or RERA portals for price data. The risk analysis below is inference only.

### Cited Findings
- Registration-linked data is already reported to FIU-IND by registrars (PTRs for ₹50 lakh+ transactions) and is otherwise in government custody. — [Compliance7](https://blog.compliance7.com/2026/04/22/fiu-ind-registration-real-estate-agents-india/)
- The UP RERA QR system was designed to give the public project-level data: approvals, QPRs, bank accounts, agents. RERA project data is therefore intended for public consumption. — [RealtyNMore](https://realtynmore.com/promoters-must-publish-project-qr-codes-in-every-brochure-and-advertisement-up-rera/)

### Inferences
- **Lower risk.** Linking to, or displaying, RERA project data that regulators publish for buyer verification, with attribution and freshness timestamps.
- **Higher risk:**
  - automated bulk scraping of registration (deed) portals in breach of their terms or technical controls, which can engage IT Act s.43 (unauthorised access or download) and s.66;
  - publishing named individual buyers' and sellers' transaction details, which raises DPDP issues and possibly defamation or privacy claims, because the publicly-available exemption may not cover data published by a state body;
  - Captcha-bypass, which would also breach this research's own safety rules.
- **Safer product approach:**
  - aggregate or anonymise price data (locality and tower-level medians, no names);
  - use official APIs or data-sharing MoUs where offered, or licensed data vendors;
  - let users fetch their own records through official portals.
- **[LAWYER REVIEW]** Required before any registration-data ingestion pipeline.

### Gaps
- No case law, MeitY or state guidance on scraping land or registration portals was found.
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Database protection.** Indian copyright protection for factual databases is thin: Eastern Book Co v D.B. Modak (2008) applies a "modicum of creativity" test. Portal terms of use and the IT Act still govern access.

## 15. Platform-level: home-loan regulation (RBI rules on DSAs / loan aggregators; Digital Lending Directions 2022 → 2025)

### Takeaway
The RBI (Digital Lending) Directions, 2025 (issued 8 May 2025) consolidate the 2022 framework and apply to digital lending by regulated entities through Lending Service Providers (LSPs) and Digital Lending Apps (DLAs). They do not carve out home loans. If the platform offers in-app loan offers from multiple banks or HFCs, it is a multi-lender LSP. From 1 November 2025 it must:
- show all matching offers neutrally, with lender name, APR, tenor, EMI and a KFS link;
- also name the lenders that did not match;
- never route funds through itself;
- store data in India with consent;
- appoint a grievance officer.

### Cited Findings
- **Dates.** Issued 8 May 2025. Mostly immediate. Multi-lender LSP provisions from 1 Nov 2025. DLAs reported on RBI's CIMS portal by 15 Jun 2025. — [Argus Partners](https://www.argus-p.com/updates/updates/rbi-digital-lending-directions-2025-an-overview/); [RBI press release via FIDC](https://www.fidcindia.org.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/RBI-DIGITAL-LENDING-PRESS-RELEASE-08-05-25.pdf)
- **LSP definition.** An agent of a regulated entity that performs digital lending functions: customer acquisition, services incidental to underwriting or pricing, servicing, monitoring, recovery. — [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/india/financial-services/1634276/digital-lending-20-breaking-down-the-rbi-digital-lending-directions-2025)
- **Multi-lender display rules.** Neutral display of all matching offers, including names of unmatched lenders, each with RE name, amount, tenor, APR, repayment and KFS link. — [Argus Partners](https://www.argus-p.com/updates/updates/rbi-digital-lending-directions-2025-an-overview/)
- **Funds, data, grievance, scope.**
  - Disbursal and repayment go directly between borrower and RE, with no pass-through.
  - Data needs express consent and is stored in India on a minimal, need-based basis. Biometrics are barred unless legally required.
  - Grievance officers are mandatory for REs and LSPs.
  - Home loans are not specifically excluded.
  — [Argus Partners](https://www.argus-p.com/updates/updates/rbi-digital-lending-directions-2025-an-overview/)

### Inferences
- **Lower risk.** A simple "talk to a bank partner" referral where the bank onboards the customer off-platform.
- **Higher risk.** An in-app eligibility check plus offers from several lenders makes the platform a multi-lender LSP. The lender partners will then impose the Directions contractually: KFS, neutrality, data localisation, grievance officer, DLA listing.
- **Fees.** Fees paid by lenders to the LSP must not be charged to borrowers. Commissions should be disclosed.

### Gaps
- **[UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND] Other RBI rules.**
  - The RBI DSA/DMA code of conduct and outsourcing directions for banks.
  - NHB/RBI rules for HFCs.
  - Housing-loan LTV caps: 90% for loans up to ₹30 lakh, 80% for ₹30–75 lakh, 75% above ₹75 lakh, with stamp duty excluded from cost for higher-value loans.

  None were verified this session.
- Whether non-digital, offline DSA referrals by a platform fall under the Digital Lending Directions or only the DSA outsourcing rules was not verified. **[LAWYER REVIEW]**
