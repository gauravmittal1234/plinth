Phase 2 specialist input for the PRD · Author: competitor benchmarking / product teardown · 18 Oct 2026

**Evidence conventions**
- **[P1-CB §n]**: Phase 1 note `research_notes/Delhi NCR AI real estate platform/competitive_benchmark.md`, section n. **[P1-R]**: Phase 1 report. **[P1-RISK]**: `research_notes/Developer and project risk scoring/ncr_risk_data_sources.md`. The URLs are in those files.

## 1. Summary

1. **Nobody shows NCR buyers how builders actually perform on delivery, so the Delivery Risk score has no direct consumer competitor.**
   - Builder pages show only counts and developer-written copy: "The builder has delivered 37 projects so far" [S2]; "Experience 23+ years" for a company the same page says started in 2014 [S11]; "12 Years Experience" against an FAQ saying the firm was established in 2007 [S19].
   - None of the five portals or four developer sites I checked shows delay history, original vs revised possession dates, or RERA complaint counts.
   - HARERA already publishes earlier and revised completion dates, QPRs and about 17.9k judgements [S32; P1-RISK].
2. **"Verified" never means legally sound or currently available.**
   - The definitions are narrow: "99acres team visits the property site to verify posting details" [S3]; "Authentic location and real photos" [S6].
   - The same platforms disclaim the data: "not physically verified" (99acres) [S2]; MagicBricks "does not verify such details" (RERA) [S13]; Housing "only acts as a medium for advertisement" [S5].
   - No portal shows title, registry, OC or dues status. That is the single biggest gap for Noida/GNW resale.
3. **The buyer's phone number is the product being sold.**
   - Housing pre-ticks "I agree to be contacted by Housing and agents via WhatsApp, SMS, phone, email" [S5]. 99acres pre-ticks "I am interested in site visits" [S3].
   - MagicBricks' T&C allow contact by "partners, and authorized agents, regardless of Do Not Disturb registration" [S13].
   - Each enquiry is sold to 4–15 brokers [P1-CB §6].
4. **The real price is hidden everywhere.**
   - Projects are priced on super area as "Onwards" or "+ Charges" [S2, S10]. Housing says outright: "Price excludes maintenance, floor rise cost, stamp duty, registration, GST etc." [S5].
   - No portal or developer site shows an all-in cost on carpet area [S2, S5, S10, S21–S24].
5. **Supply data is visibly dirty.** *J: this is the cheapest place to look obviously better.*
   - "New projects" with possession in Dec 2013 [S4], and a broker-created "project" page [S4].
   - "Carpet Area 1 sq.ft" [S16], and units mixed up ("Super Area 1031 sqm" at ₹1,171/sqft) [S9].
   - 18 of 26 NoBroker results carried an inactive reason (sold out, incorrect, timed out) while still active [S15].
6. **"Contact the developer" usually means contacting a broker.**
   - Housing project pages list "Sellers you may contact": three brokerages, not M3M [S5].
   - 99acres' FAQ names "TIMES INTERNET LIMITED" (MagicBricks' parent) among the "major brokers" for a project [S2].
   - Broker-led cards carry a "Zero Brokerage" badge [S4].
7. **After booking, buyers are on their own.**
   - Portals stop at the lead.
   - Developer buyer apps are weak. Godrej's is rated 3.3★ ("payments page keeps looping") [S25]. DLF's latest public construction update was Jan 2026 [S21]. Signature Global takes EOI and instalment money through a legacy ASP.NET form [S24].
8. **Incumbents' consumer AI is shallow; the developer-side AI is ahead of it.**
   - Consumer-side: AI-generated project summaries (MagicBricks [S10]), "generated by system (SIA)" listing text (NoBroker [S16]), Housing search inside ChatGPT (Jun 2026) [S7], and an unverified "~98% accurate" AVM [S14].
   - Developer-side vendors already sell 10-language voice agents that call a lead within 30 seconds (Sell.Do) [S26].
9. **Aurum now owns a closed loop and is the most dangerous fast-follower.**
   - Housing.com is the demand side. PropTiger (with PropPartnerX co-broking) is brokerage [S20]. Sell.Do, "Backed by Aurum PropTech", is the developer CRM, CP and launch stack [S26].
   - *J:* Aurum can wire portal leads straight into developer CRMs.
10. **Where to win.** Match the commoditised search toolkit at launch (Section 2.7). Win on five things no one does:
    - a delivery-risk and RERA truth panel
    - layered verification with expiry and registry status
    - all-in cost on carpet area
    - consent-first, capped contact
    - a WhatsApp-native post-enquiry and post-booking companion

## 2. Analysis

### 2.1 Platform teardowns

#### 99acres (Info Edge)
- **Discovery**
  - Gurgaon new-projects list: 1,546 projects, with filters for RERA-registered, budget, BHK, possession year (2021–2034), locality, builder and property type [S1].
  - A microphone icon appears in the web search bar. *J:* this is voice input; the languages supported are unknown.
  - GNW resale: 10,899 results. Owner/Dealer labels, a "Verified" toggle, an "In-focus" paid tag and a "4 people already contacted this week" nudge [S3].
- **Project page** (Signature Global Titanium SPR) [S2]
  - Visible: a modal disclaimer, badges ("RERA", "No Brokerage", "3D Floor Plans Available"), "Completion in May, 2031", and "₹5.29–7.19 Cr + Charges" on super built-up area only.
  - Also: specifications, amenities, a locality rating of 3.6/5 from 4 reviews, resale units by owners, and a payment-plan download.
  - The RERA number appears only inside SEO text.
  - The builder block is a blurb plus a project count.
  - No construction progress, all-in cost or delay history.
- **Contact flow** [S3]
  - The modal shows the owner's full name and a masked number.
  - It asks "Are you a property dealer?" and for name and phone ("This number would be verified").
  - It promises "shared with this advertiser only".
  - "Site visits" is pre-ticked.
- **Seller side**
  - One free listing, paid visibility plans, and a "BOSS" plan that sells brokers access to owners who want to sell [P1-CB §2; P1 seller notes].
  - Reviews: "10 calls per day suggesting to make payment" [S8].
- **Ratings**: 4.8★ Play (2.16L), Trustpilot 1.2 [S8; P1-CB §5].
- **Verdict (*J*)**: strongest content depth and SEO. Every trust signal is either self-declared or about the posting, not the property.

#### MagicBricks (Times Internet)
- **Discovery**
  - Gurgaon: 22,885 flats. Filters include "Posted By". Sorting by price and rate per sq ft.
  - Super and carpet area are mixed across cards [S9].
  - Server-side flags show premium-only and "fresh property locked" listings [S9]. *J:* contact is gated behind MB Prime ("Contact upto 30 Owners directly") [S9].
- **Project page** (Whiteland Urban Resort, Dwarka Expressway) [S10]
  - RERA IDs, with the HARERA portal named as the source.
  - Possession "Sep'31". Super area only, "₹6.88 Cr Onwards".
  - "Project Summary is Ai Generated".
  - Rating 3.7 from 5 ratings.
  - Quarterly price trend, project vs locality: project −1%, Sector 103 −9% quarter on quarter.
  - Loan block "Offers from 34+ Banks".
  - Builder box: "Total Projects 4 / Completed 1".
  - Every listed unit inside the project is from an agent.
- **Other features**
  - The app lists a free site-visit cab, QPR/RERA certificates on MagicHomes, and PropWorth [P1-CB §1].
  - PropWorth claims "~98% accurate … ±2%" with no published method [S14].
- **Seller side**
  - Ad packages, a "Top Spot Visibility Package", and "iAdvantage" credits to view buyer contacts [S12].
  - T&C: "subscription fee is non-refundable"; "50% of the total order value" is treated as an activation charge [S13].
- **Ratings**: 4.4★ Play, Trustpilot 1.1. Reviews mention deactivated paid plans and "no place to track dates of the new leads" [S8; P1-CB §5].
- **Verdict (*J*)**: the most new-project utilities (cab, QPR, AVM). The T&C are the most hostile to users (DND override, forfeiture).

#### Housing.com (Aurum PropTech since Sep 2026; with PropTiger, Sell.Do)
- **Discovery**
  - The homepage claims "80K+ total verified" [S6].
  - Gurgaon "New Projects" holds 2,708 entries, of which 1,593 are ready to move. It includes DLF The Belaire, possession Dec 2013 [S4].
  - Cards lead with broker names ("ARS LANDBASE + 1 more") alongside "Zero Brokerage".
  - "Recommended Sellers" shows one broker with "2025 Yrs Experience" [S4].
  - A festival banner is inserted in the results [S4].
  - The listing page claims "100% Verified Properties" [S6].
- **Project page** (M3M Golf Hills Ph 1) [S5]
  - "Rera Id … Check RERA Status" link-out.
  - The explicit exclusions line quoted in the Summary (maintenance, floor rise, stamp duty, registration, GST).
  - 2D/3D plans with room dimensions.
  - Price trend: project +3.12% vs locality −8.84% over one year.
  - Reviews: 1 review rating everything 5/5.
  - Developer block: "2010 Year estd. / 79 Projects" plus the developer's own first-person copy.
  - Q&A: a rent question is answered with an appreciation figure.
  - Contact goes to three brokerages.
- **Contact flow**: the modal says "You have a fine taste. This property is great!" Consent to contact by Housing *and agents* on every channel is pre-ticked [S5].
- **AI**: property search inside ChatGPT since 11 Jun 2026 [S7]. The web homepage shows no AI entry point [S6].
- **Ratings**: 4.4★ Play. Reviews say "free for one day", report 3+ sales calls a day, and say owner numbers are gated behind paid membership [S8].
- **Verdict (*J*)**: the cleanest visual design, but the worst inventory hygiene and the most aggressive default consent. As part of Aurum, it is the best placed to integrate with developers.

#### NoBroker
- **Discovery**
  - The search page is a client-rendered shell with a list/map toggle, metro search and "Premium Filters" [S15].
  - The public API returned 10,177 Gurgaon resale homes. The page-1 cards were all `sponsored:true`, `verified:false`, and 18 of 26 carried TIMED_OUT, INCORRECT or SOLDOUT reasons [S15].
- **Listing page** [S16]
  - "₹62 Lacs Non-negotiable", plus "Verified Availability" and "Schedule Visit" buttons.
  - A report option ("Listed by Broker / Sold Out / Wrong Info").
  - Livability and transit scores. "Carpet Area 1 sq.ft".
  - System-generated text ("SIA").
  - Cross-sells: legal, agreement, loan, painting, interiors.
- **Plans** [S17]
  - Seller (3 months, + GST): ₹7,499, ₹13,499, ₹16,499 and ₹20,999 ("Guaranteed buyers or Moneyback").
  - Buyer: ₹1,899 for 25 contacts; ₹1,999 for 50 contacts plus an expert who "Negotiates price on your behalf"; ₹4,999 "Guaranteed property or 100% Refund".
- **Ratings**: 4.7★ Play. Reviews mention "no permanently delete account option" and paid batches of 25 contacts that were wrong or rented out, with only 3 replacements [S8].
- **Verdict (*J*)**: the only player selling *outcomes* to consumers (expert, moneyback). Its data freshness undermines the promise.

### 2.2 Builder and project risk information buyers see today

| Signal | Where it exists | Strength (*J*) |
|---|---|---|
| RERA number | All portals and developer sites; Square Yards adds a QR [S2, S5, S10, S19, S21–24] | Table stakes, but it is often only text, and Square Yards mislabels it [S19] |
| Possession date | Everywhere | **Misleading**: only the current promise is shown. A past date ("Mar, 2024") is shown with no delay flag [S4] |
| Builder track record | Counts and years on 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing and Square Yards [S2, S5, S11, S19] | **Very weak**: inconsistent numbers, developer-written copy, no on-time measure |
| Construction progress | MagicBricks links to RERA reports and broker YouTube videos [S10]; developer pages (stale) [S21, S23] | Weak; no structured % completion |
| Complaints / litigation | None on NCR portals. Housiey and Propsoch outside NCR [S30] | **Absent** |
| Ratings | 1 to 12 ratings per project, many from "Guest User" or "Others" [S5, S10, S19] | Statistically meaningless |
| Raw regulator data | HARERA: revised completion dates, percentage completion, QPRs, lapsed list (~230 rows), defaulter list (4 rows), ~17.9k judgements. UP RERA is behind CAPTCHAs [S32; P1-RISK] | Rich but unusable by consumers |
| Institutional ratings | Liases Foras developer ratings; CRISIL grading (historical) [S31; P1-RISK] | Paywalled or B2B |

**Assessment.** NCR buyers today get marketing, not risk information. *J:* Our score needs three things to be credible: (1) original vs revised dates and complaint counts drawn from HARERA, (2) entity resolution across a promoter's SPVs, since judgements are keyed by respondent name [S32], and (3) visible sourcing on every fact. Gurugram can launch on HARERA alone. Noida needs extra work on UP RERA CAPTCHAs.

### 2.6 Lessons from the developer and CP side

- Developers already pay for de-duplication, CP tagging with dispute resolution, collection-gated payouts, EOI tooling and AI voice qualification [S26–S29]. Our edge for developers is **better buyers**, not another CRM. That means pre-qualified, consented, risk-informed buyers delivered into *their* CRM with an appointment attached.
- Aurum's ownership of Housing, PropTiger and Sell.Do [S20, S26] is the competitive threat. *J:* Assume Aurum can offer developers "portal lead → CRM → CP payout" in one contract within 12 months. Our counter is buyer trust, which Aurum's lead-sale model structurally cannot offer.
- CP apps are poorly rated (3.3–3.9★) and CP reviews complain about junk leads [S25, S29]. *J:* A broker/CP app that ranks partners on responsiveness and closings, and routes them fewer, better leads, is a credible P1 supply-side hook.

### 2.8 Anti-patterns to avoid

| Anti-pattern | Seen at | Our rule |
|---|---|---|
| Selling one enquiry to many brokers | All portals [P1-CB §6] | Publish a share cap; the buyer chooses recipients |
| Pre-ticked or bundled consent | Housing, 99acres, Godrej, Signature Global [S3, S5, S22, S24] | Every consent box starts unticked, one purpose per box (DPDP; CCPA dark-pattern guidelines) |
| DND-override clauses | MagicBricks T&C; Sell.Do form [S13, S26] | Never in our forms |
| Flattery and false urgency | "You have a fine taste", "4 people already contacted this week" [S3, S5] | No manufactured urgency. Show real counts only if they are true and useful |
| Contact paywalls with hidden caps or "threshold limits" | Housing, MagicBricks, NoBroker [S8, S9, S17] | Contact is free for buyers. Monetise outcomes |
| Prepaid, non-refundable seller plans | MagicBricks (50% forfeiture), NoBroker refund disputes [S13; P1-CB §5] | Pay on success; automatic, rule-based refunds |
| Badges without definitions ("100% Verified") | Housing, Square Yards [S6, S18] | Every badge links to its standard and date |
| Broker inventory dressed as developer or owner supply | Housing, Square Yards [S4, S5, S18] | Label every counterparty and its incentive |
| Developer copy presented as platform information | 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing builder blocks [S2, S5, S11] | Label "developer says" separately from "records show" |
| Stale, reactivated or mis-categorised listings | NoBroker, Housing [S4, S15] | Expire automatically; no reactivation without re-verification |
| Unverifiable accuracy claims | PropWorth "~98%" [S14] | Publish error by segment, or don't claim accuracy |
| Unlabelled, error-prone AI text | Auto-descriptions contradicting listed price [S3, S16] | Label AI text, generate from structured data, run consistency checks |
| No permanent account deletion | NoBroker [S8] | One-tap delete, plus a "who has my number" log |
| Sales calls to owners to upsell | 99acres, Housing [S8] | No outbound upsell calls. In-product, opt-in only |
| Blocking modals, timed and exit popups, gated brochures | 99acres, Godrej, M3M [S2, S22, S23] | Show value before asking for anything |

## 4. Open questions for the founder

1. **Broker inventory at launch.** Do we list broker/CP inventory at all, or only verified developer projects, KYC'd owners and RERA-registered CPs under our cap and labelling rules? This determines supply depth vs trust.
2. **Money from the rated.** Will we take developer CP commission or lead fees while publishing their Delivery Risk band? If so, what firewall and disclosure do we commit to publicly?
3. **The contact cap.** Is it 1, 2 or 3 recipients per enquiry? Is buyer contact always free?
4. **How far "verified" goes, and who signs.** Do we stop at availability and ownership documents, or extend to registry and dues with lawyer sign-off and indemnity cover from day one?
5. **Launch scope.** Gurugram UC only, where R1 and R2 can be built on HARERA, or Gurugram UC plus GNW resale, where R6 and R12 become P0 and need UP data work?
6. **Data budget.** Do we license registry transaction data (PropEquity, Liases Foras, Square Yards) before launch, or ship R10 later from scraped and partner data?
7. **Channel primacy.** Is WhatsApp the primary interface, with the app for depth, or the reverse? Does this change what "Android-first" means?
8. **Right of reply.** What is the SLA, who arbitrates a developer's dispute of a fact, and does a disputed fact stay visible meanwhile?
9. **The Aurum stack.** Integrate with Sell.Do and LeadSquared to reach developers, or avoid feeding a competitor's ecosystem?
10. **Voice.** Is Hinglish voice part of the MVP, or text-first with voice in P1?
11. **Seller fee model.** What pay-on-success fee (percentage or flat), and does it start before RERA agent registration in Haryana and UP is complete?
