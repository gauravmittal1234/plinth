## 1. Summary

1. **The business model decides our obligations.** RERA's "real estate agent" covers anyone who, for remuneration, "introduces, through any medium, prospective buyers and sellers to each other" [V: s.2(zm)]. The first commission, lead fee or success fee on a registered project makes us an agent in that state, and at about ₹20 lakh agency turnover a PMLA reporting entity [R]. Under the recommended hybrid model (CP commission from M12; resale and loans M4–M6), **register in Haryana and UP before the first paid deal** [J].
2. **Haryana is fast; UP is slow.** HARERA Gurugram charges a company **₹2,50,000** and grants within **30 days**; renewal needs a **quarterly register of commission % per deal** [V]. UP's Rules also list ₹2,50,000, but a **5 Jan 2026 order** adds **4-day classroom training plus an exam (₹6,000 per employee)** before you can apply; UP also needs an office in the state and police/criminal-case affidavits [V]. Defer Delhi.
3. **Listings become a regulatory gate.** No unregistered projects [V: s.3, s.10(a)]. RERA number plus authority website on every project surface [V: s.11(2)]; in Haryana **bold, top right**, and spoken aloud in audio/video [V: HARERA Dir. 3/2021]. UP QR code and Haryana DTCP licence [R]. Our own agent number quoted in every sale [V: s.9(5)].
4. **Risk scores are our own publication, with no intermediary shield.** They ship only with the guardrail stack [R]: naming, methodology, facts vs opinion, right of reply, evidence vault, conflicts firewall, media-liability cover. As an agent we also must not "falsely represent… a particular standard or grade" [V: s.10(c)(i)(A)].
5. **AI staging, renders, video and synthetic voice are synthetically generated information** under the IT Rules (in force 20 Feb 2026): visible label plus embedded provenance [R]. A structure lock and "show original" toggle defend against RERA s.12/s.10 and consumer-law misrepresentation [V/J].
6. **Outbound calling tightened on 18 Sep 2026** (TCCCPR Third Amendment) [V]. AI/robo (A2P) calls must be pre-declared to the telco or count as spam. Enquiry-based follow-up is allowed **for 7 days only**, with the enquiry held in verifiable form. Promotional calls use 140xx. Launch **inbound-first and WhatsApp-first**, and keep the WhatsApp bot property-specific: Meta bars general-purpose AI assistants [V].
7. **DPDP:** consent managers from **13 Nov 2026**; full obligations from **13 May 2027** [R]. Itemised per-recipient lead consent and a "who has my number" dashboard are both compliance and our core trust promise.
8. **Never touch client money.** Tokens and bookings go direct to the seller, the RERA collection account or a bank escrow; we collect only our own fees via an authorised payment aggregator [J/U]. Loans: single-lender referral first; showing several lenders' offers triggers RBI multi-lender LSP rules (from 1 Nov 2025) [R].
9. **Documents: "red flags with page citations", never "clear title".** Title opinions come from independent advocates (BCI Rules 36–37; Madras HC 2024 denied lawyer-listing platforms safe harbour) [R]. The ₹6,999 lawyer-signed "Verified Flat Check" needs a structuring opinion (Q16).
10. **Compliance is also product:** RERA and agent status checks, OC/registry status, a true-cost calculator (stamp duty, registration, GST, TDS s.393(1)), state checklists, the s.13 "max 10% before registered agreement" check and a consent dashboard. Cheap to build from public data; together they are the trust story.

**Who registers.** The company, as "other than individual". Every partner broker or CP we route deals to needs **their own** state registration, and the broker app must check it against the published agent lists (UP showed 7,376 agents on 18 Oct 2026) [V/J].

**Agent obligations** (s.9, s.10; UP r.13) [V]: never facilitate unregistered projects; keep books, records and documents; no unfair trade practices (false "standard or grade", false approval or affiliation, misleading representations, ads for services not offered); give allottees their documents at booking; help allottee and promoter exercise their rights; quote our registration number in every sale. **Penalty:** ₹10,000/day up to 5% of unit cost (s.62) [V].

**Display [J]:** the relevant state's agent number in the footer, project pages, WhatsApp profile, ad creatives, invoices, booking forms and the AI call opening, plus a per-state registration page.

**Resale nuance.** Section 9(1) bites on units "being sold by the promoter" in a registered project [V], so owner-to-owner resale brokerage may sit **outside s.9**. But UP RERA's manual calls registration "mandatory for all brokers and facilitators operating within the State" [V]. Treat resale as needing registration until counsel says otherwise (Q1) [J].

### 2.3 PMLA and FIU-IND

The PMLA regulator is the **DG Audit (CBIC)**, which issued "Guidelines for Reporting Entities (Real Estate Agents)" (dated 29 Nov; year illegible in the scan) [V]. Supervision is live: in Sept 2026 HARERA forwarded to agents a DG Audit/MHA notice of a new UAPA terrorist designation, instructing them to screen clients, refuse dealings, apply enhanced due diligence and report [V].

### 2.4 Platform-wide obligations

- **IT Act intermediary** (listings, reviews, chat) [R]: published grievance officer resolving within **7 days** (cut from 15 by the Feb 2026 amendment); takedown within **3 hours** of a court or government order; published rules and privacy policy with regular user notices; SGI duties (§3).
- **E-Commerce Rules 2020** [R/U]: grievance officer details; explicit consent with no pre-ticked boxes (r.4(9), as cited by the CCPA); seller details; no price manipulation.
- **CCPA** [R]: Dark Patterns Guidelines (30 Nov 2023, 13 patterns) and the **June 2025 self-audit and declaration advisory**; Misleading Advertisements Guidelines (9 Jun 2022), penalties up to ₹10 lakh (₹50 lakh for repeats).
- **Until 13 May 2027** IT Act s.43A and the SPDI Rules 2011 still apply [U]; build to DPDP now.
- **Accessibility:** RPwD Act 2016 (ss.40, 46); Supreme Court pressure for mandatory standards (*Rajive Raturi*, Nov 2024; *Amar Jain*, Apr 2025, on accessible digital KYC) [U]. Target WCAG 2.1 AA / IS 17802 [J].

## 6. Questions for a lawyer (and a CA where marked)

1. **RERA perimeter.** Under s.2(zm) and s.9(1) and the HARERA, UP RERA and Delhi rules, which need agent registration: (a) free listings; (b) per-lead or per-visit fees from promoters; (c) booking success fees; (d) flat developer SaaS fees; (e) brokerage on owner-sold completed resale flats? Does UP RERA's "all brokers and facilitators" wording reach (e)? Do unpaid beta introductions become "facilitation" once we charge?
2. **Haryana.** Does one HARERA Gurugram registration cover HARERA Panchkula projects (Faridabad), given s.9(3)(a)'s "single registration… for the entire State"? If not, which fees and forms apply?
3. **UP training.** Under the 5 Jan 2026 order, which staff must train: only "agency work" staff, or also ops staff supervising AI agents? May AI agents hold UP sales conversations? What are the exemption criteria?
4. **UP validity and fees.** r.10(3) says 10 years, Form H says 5. Which applies? Have the 2016 agent fees been amended?
5. **Delhi.** Current agent fees, forms, validity and documents at Delhi RERA? Is registration needed for Delhi resale-only brokerage?
6. **PMLA.** Is the ₹20 lakh threshold measured on total turnover or agency receipts? Register on FINGate from the first fee or on crossing the threshold? Is CDD needed on buyer **and** seller when only the developer pays us? What is the date and status of the DG Audit guidelines? May we use DigiLocker or offline Aadhaar, given Aadhaar authentication needs s.11A notification?
7. **Scores while an agent.** Does grading developers who pay us commissions create exposure under RERA s.10(c)(i)(A) or CPA s.2(47)(j)? Would a separate scoring entity with an editorial firewall strengthen fair-comment, good-faith and *Bloomberg v Zee* defences? Please approve the disclaimer wording.
8. **Data.** Do HARERA, UP RERA, MCA21 and eCourts terms permit low-rate automated collection (IT Act s.43)? Does the government copyright clause "not… derogatory… or misleading context" expose negative grades built on RERA data? Does DPDP s.3(c)(ii) cover promoter and director data from RERA and MCA?
9. **SGI.** Is staging a real flat SGI under r.2(1)(wa)? Are we an SGI-tool provider when sellers self-serve, and a publisher when our team stages? What label size and placement and which metadata standard satisfy "prominent" and "permanent"?
10. **Developer use of our renders.** Who bears RERA s.12 and consumer-law liability, and which indemnities and usage terms should we require?
11. **TCCCPR Third Amendment 2026.** Effective dates? Does a web or WhatsApp enquiry start the 7-day window, and what is "verifiable form"? How do we register legacy consents? Is a requested call-back promotional or service? Does inbound-only AI voice need an A2P declaration?
12. **Call recording.** Is notice enough, or is express consent needed for AI and human calls? Can DPDP "legitimate uses" cover quality and dispute purposes?
13. **DPDP roles.** Are lead recipients (brokers, developers, lenders) our processors or independent fiduciaries? What consent artefact covers several named recipients? What is our SDF risk? Must we honour consents arriving via consent managers?
14. **Loans.** Is a single-lender referral after an in-app eligibility estimate LSP activity? What is the current citation of the Digital Lending Directions after RBI's late-2025 consolidation [U]? May we earn lender fees and brokerage on the same deal?
15. **Escrow.** Can tokens go into a bank-administered escrow where we are a party but not the account holder, without PA authorisation (PSS Act s.4)? Can our fee be paid from it?
16. **Verified Flat Check (₹6,999).** If AI extracts issues, a panel advocate signs and we pay the advocate a flat fee, is that practising law (Advocates Act ss.29/33), fee-sharing or solicitation (BCI r.36–37)? Is "user engages the advocate directly; we charge a software fee" compliant?
17. **Badges.** What minimum checks and wording keep "Registry-ready", "RERA-verified" and "Owner-verified" non-misleading under CPA s.2(28) and the 2022 Guidelines? What is our liability if a badged flat fails?
18. **Reviews.** Is IS 19000:2022 voluntary for us? What is our exposure for resident reviews alleging builder misconduct, and how do we handle disclosure orders?
19. **(CA) GST.** Does the UP office need a UP GSTIN? What is the place of supply for brokerage, report and referral income? Are we an e-commerce operator under CGST s.52 if we collect partner brokers' fees?
20. **NRI.** What are the TDS section, rates and forms under the IT Act 2025 for NRI sellers, and the lower-deduction certificate process? Are there limits on our role in POA execution, and FEMA reporting duties?
21. **Discrimination.** Is it lawful to host "vegetarian only", religion or caste filters and listings? What is our liability?
22. **Insurance.** What limits and exclusions do we need on media-liability and PI cover (criminal-defamation defence costs, regulatory fines, DPDP penalties)?
