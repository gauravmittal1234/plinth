What’s in it for you?

Your enquiry goes to one agent the buyer chose, not fifteen. The buyer arrives knowing the records, and nobody can buy their way above you.

What you get

  1. Buyers who chose you. One agent per buyer and flat. We never sell or broadcast an enquiry.
  2. Buyers who already know. The delivery record, the all-in cost and the paperwork status. Fewer wasted visits.
  3. Proof you introduced them. Every consented contact and verified visit is time-stamped.
  4. Paid within 7 days of registration on desk deals, with the TDS and GST paperwork done.
  5. Your mandates kept yours. We don’t approach an owner you hold a mandate for.
  6. Free tools on WhatsApp. List by forwarding a message; confirm every flat in one weekly tap.

What we ask

  • Your HARERA agent number, PAN and an office address.
  • The owner’s OTP for every mandate.
  • Honest availability, confirmed every week.
  • Respect for a buyer’s STOP and quiet hours.
  • Telling buyers who pays you.

What our own staff do

Plinth’s staff explain records and handle desk paperwork. They don’t show flats or negotiate. The field work is yours.

What we never do

  • Sell your leads, your listings or your data.
  • Take developer money, directly or through you.
  • Show anyone a band before buyers see it.
  • Charge for a badge, a ranking or a placement.
  • Hold your listings hostage. Leave anytime and export them.

Who owns an introduction?

You do, once the buyer taps to connect you and makes a verified visit to that flat. It holds for 60 days after the last verified visit, and never past 90 days after the first.

A verified visit means the buyer’s own OTP at the flat plus a location check. A claim can’t be planted from a desk.

The contact cap works for you too. A buyer lets at most 3 new parties reach them in 30 days, and you count once, however many flats you show them. Listing agents you co-broke with don’t count.

  1. An introduction is a consented contact plus a verified visit, for one flat.
  2. It is yours for 60 days from the last verified visit, and never more than 90 days from the first.
  3. Claiming an earlier introduction made off Plinth? Bring dated evidence: a message, a visit slip or a call log.
  4. The buyer may switch agents at any time. The ledger then gives 25% of the agent share to the agent who introduced them. The buyer never pays twice.
  5. Disputes are decided within 7 days. Appeals go to a panel that includes one outside broker.
  6. You can export your own ledger as evidence, whenever you like.

Will we poach your owners?

No. While your mandate is active, and for 60 days after it ends, we don’t approach that owner to list directly or to use our desk.

  • An owner who comes to us on their own sees who holds the mandate, and until when.
  • One flat, one card. The owner’s listing and yours merge; nobody relists it without a mandate.
  • The owner can end a mandate at any time. That is their right, and we won’t argue it for you.

Are we a channel partner?

No, and we never will be. We never take any part of a commission a developer pays you.

Your client can name you.
When a buyer books a site visit through Plinth, they can name their agent. The site office receives “Agent of record” with the booking.
We are never anyone’s lead source.
We never register with a developer as a source. A buyer who names no agent is booked as a direct visitor.
Tagging stays between you and the developer.
We don’t decide who tagged a client first. If your client wants, they can share a dated first-contact receipt from Plinth.
The developer is the seller, not our partner.
We score their projects from public records. They pay us nothing, and so do you. Keep every empanelment you have.

Bands read the same public records you would check before empanelling with a developer: progress against time, filings, orders and the group’s finished projects. Use them however helps your business.

Can I share a risk card?

Yes. Share the live link. It always shows today’s band and date, so it never goes stale in a client’s chat.

  • Send the link, not a screenshot, and never crop out or cover the band.
  • Don’t write “Plinth recommends”. We don’t recommend projects or agents.

  • We count how many people open your links, for you to see. Opens never change a band or a ranking.

By verification, then freshness. Nothing else. Verification is free, and nobody can buy a place, including you.
