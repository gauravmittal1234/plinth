How is my number masked?

Calls and WhatsApp messages pass through a relay number. The other side never sees yours unless you tap to share it.

  1. You choose. You tap Contact and choose who may reach you, for one purpose.
  2. We relay. We connect you through a relay number. Your own number is never shared without your tap; after a confirmed visit you can tap Share my number.
  3. It ends. The relay stays alive while a deal is active, and ends 30 days after your last message unless you renew. A deal is active from your first visit to registration. You can end any relay sooner by revoking it.
