What to do next

  1. Ask for the registration number on their certificate, and search for it here.
  2. Check the regulator’s own site: HARERA agents or UP RERA agents.
  3. Still nothing? You can keep talking to them. Just pay a booking amount only to the seller or the project’s RERA collection account, never to an agent.

Who pays the agent?

Ask before your first visit. Every agent on Plinth carries a label that answers it.

On a new launch
The developer usually pays the agent if you book. You pay them nothing.
On a resale flat
The seller pays their agent. If you hire an agent too, you pay yours, once.
What Plinth gets
Nothing from developers, ever. Partner agents pay us nothing to appear here. On resale deals through our desk, we share our own fee with the agent who did the field work.

Anyone who sells a unit from the developer in a RERA-registered project must be registered (RERA Act, section 9). For older resale flats the rule is less clear, so read registration as a good sign rather than a must.

What does “Plinth partner” mean?

Verified, never endorsed. We checked their registration, PAN and office, and they agreed to our published rules.

It doesn’t mean we vouch for a project they sell, or for their advice. Their delivery bands are the same as everyone’s, and nobody can pay us to appear here or to rank higher.
