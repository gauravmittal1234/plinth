What exactly do we predict?

Whether a phase gets its OC more than 12 months after its original RERA date. We call this Late12.

We add extensions given to every project, such as COVID and the 4-month MoHUA extension of Jul 2026. We don’t add extensions granted to one project. Those are part of the delay we predict.

Where does the data come from?

Public registers first. Licensed vendors only where a register has no clean public feed.

We recompute within 72 hours of a new filing or order. Data more than 120 days old gets a stale flag.

Two AI extractors read each order and PDF separately. If they disagree, a person decides. Developer evidence is labelled, and moves a score only once we verify it.
