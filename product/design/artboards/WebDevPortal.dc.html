What a notice does, and doesn’t

  • We acknowledge it within 48 hours, with a case reference.
  • Within 5 working days you get our standard evidence pack: every source behind your pages, its snapshot date and hash, the method version, and your dispute history.
  • Our counsel replies in writing within 15 working days.
  • A notice doesn’t pause or change a band. Facts do, through the lanes above.
  • If a court orders it, the page switches to facts-only: the band is hidden, every sourced fact stays, and the page says why.
